# Booster Rocket v1 — Roadmap

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-booster-rocket-v1-design.md` (later specs in that folder override parts of it)
**Visual system:** `DESIGN.md`, `design/reference/`
**Rules for agents:** `AGENTS.md`

This page is the status of the build. Update it in the same commit that finishes a step.

## Decisions from the owner (2026-11-14)

1. **Scope is the whole v1 spec.** Nothing is cut for a first release.
2. **Forms are signed in Documenso from the start**, as the spec describes.
3. **Parents edit their phone and address.** The portal writes to QuickBooks; the read-only rule is lifted for the write paths named below.
4. **A parent stays signed in for 90 days, renewed by use.** Every request still re-checks QuickBooks links and blocks.
5. **The portal can be installed as an app** on iOS and Android, as well as used as a website. This was not in the v1 spec.
6. **The portal's address is set by whoever installs it.** No hostname is written into the code, and nothing depends on one hosting provider.
7. **Work is committed locally on the branch `parent-portal`.** Nothing is pushed or deployed until the owner says so.

Facts from the owner: Intuit's production app assessment has not been started. The real books have Family, Student and Parent customer types; not every parent has their own email address. Invoices are sent with online ACH payment on.

## Decisions from the owner (2026-11-16)

8. **Checking in from inside the installed app is a lower priority, and nothing is built for it now**: no code to type in and no scanner inside the app. Scanning the shift's QR code with the phone's camera stays the way to check in. On an iPhone that opens Safari, not the installed app, because Apple lets only native apps capture a link; a volunteer whose Safari has no session signs in there once and is brought back to the check-in page. The owner may later move from the app installed from the browser to brandable native iOS and Android apps, which would bring link capture with them (Later, if wanted, below).

## How the work is done

One slice at a time, each small enough to see on a screen, with a short brief instead of a long plan. Tests are written with the code. The privacy gates are automatic (`AGENTS.md`). A security review is added where a slice writes to QuickBooks, changes sign-in, shows money or stores new personal data. Each step ends with the full suite green and a local commit. Slices in the same step are built in parallel in separate worktrees and merged at the end of the step.

## Checks the spec asked for before building (v1 spec §15)

The QuickBooks rows below are answered by the check of the portal's requests (step 7: Settings, QuickBooks, **Check the portal's requests**, `/staff/settings/quickbooks/check`). Its rows are the expectations each of these rows listed before, kept in one place in the code (`src/lib/quickbooks/sandbox-check/expectations.ts`, beside the request strings in `src/lib/quickbooks/client.ts`, with `minorversion=75`). Until an Admin has run it against an Intuit sandbox company, each stays open. It was run once, on 2026-11-17, on the proof-of-concept host against an Intuit sandbox company: 34 of its 37 rows answered as expected. The three others: "Making a family inactive" was refused with code 800 (the check's clean-up then made all five of its records inactive, students and parents first, so QuickBooks refuses a family while a customer under it is active); "Making a customer with an open balance inactive" was refused with code 6220; and 8 of 9 invoices carried a Pay link, the one without being the check's own, made as a buy-out invoice is made. The check now expects 800 and 6220 and adds a Pay link probe (an invoice with a bill-to email address), and the portal makes a family inactive in one action, everyone under it first, never anyone with a balance. It was run again the same day at 6:03 PM, with these changes, against the same sandbox company: every request answered as the portal expects. Its two open questions were answered: made without an email address the invoice came back with no Pay link, and made carrying a bill-to email address it came back with one, its email status still not set (so QuickBooks gives a Pay link only to an invoice that carries an email address); and making the customer under a family inactive left the family's own version as it was. The owner then decided that a buy-out invoice carries, as its bill-to email, the sign-in address of the guardian who chose it (nothing is emailed). The rows below are answered in an Intuit sandbox, not yet against a club's own books, and each keeps what is left for a person by hand (runbook, part 2). ## Known gaps

Left by the review of step 2 (2026-11-15):

- **No credit line on a parent's page for a family with other customers under it.** QuickBooks' `BalanceWithJobs` counts every customer under the family, so the parent's credit line is shown only when the roster holds nothing else there. Since step 4 the worker's daily read compares each customer's own balance with its invoices for every family, and Balances lists the ones that differ.
- **Payments and Home ask the roster first; `loadFamilyMoney()` alone does not.** It refuses an inactive family and a row that isn't the family's, but not a roster cache from another company than the connected one, or a broken shape: a new page that shows money must call `loadMyFamily()` first (`AGENTS.md`, Money).
- **Figures go "not current" when the portal is only busy.** When every place for a live read is taken for 2 seconds, or another copy of the portal holds the family's lock or the connection row, the family sees its last figures with Pay switched off for up to 30 seconds. The note no longer blames an outage; waiting for the read under way would be better.
- **A code step left on a shared device** is still picked up by the next visitor for its 10 minutes: they see "we've sent a code" and can ask for it to be sent again, until they choose "Use a different email", which now hands them nothing of the earlier person.

Left by step 3 (2026-11-15):

- **A phone saved while the roster lock is held shows the old phone in the details box for a few minutes.** The save no longer writes the cache under a refresh, or under a staff change that holds the lock: it asks the worker for another read (step 7, `890e0b9`), which brings the new phone in at its next wake, and the parent is told the Family page will catch up in a few minutes. QuickBooks is right throughout, and the edit page reads it live.
- **The field limits for a parent's phone and address are cautious guesses** (phone 30; address lines 200; city, region and country 100; postal code 20): Intuit's reference could not be read when they were chosen. The sandbox check above settles them.
- **Coverage is everyone or a chosen list.** The spec's filter by grade or section has no data behind it (Waiting on the owner). A due date sends reminders for every kind: forms on their schedule, volunteering, and payments and checklist items 14 and 3 days before (step 8, `17968b1`), with no schedule of their own for the last two. There is no way to delete a whole season (staff stop showing it and remove its steps). Requirements are reordered within a step since step 7 (`a82ee6b`).
- **Opening the audit log is not audited**, even filtered to one person's record; the export is. Say if a filtered view should be.
- **The backup test needs Postgres's own tools on the CI runner.** Closed by step 7: CI names the Postgres service's container in `BR_TEST_DB_CONTAINER`, so the test runs `pg_dump`, `pg_restore` and `psql` inside it, the server's own version, as `deploy/` does. A runner's own client could be older than the server, or new enough to write settings Postgres 18 refuses. Not yet seen on GitHub (checks table).
- **A header the proxy doesn't send still puts the staff door in one shared bucket.** With `CLIENT_IP_HEADER=none` Better Auth is told there is no address and its limiter stands aside, and the portal's own per-IP sign-in limits are skipped whenever the address is unknown. But when a header is named and never arrives, Better Auth (which can't be told per request) counts every visitor together: three staff sign-in requests in ten seconds for the whole club. The Health page's parent line says so once the latest sign-ins have no address; the fix is the right header.
- **An untouched address part that is too long is sent again when the parent edits another part.** A phone-only save never sends the address, and only changed fields are checked, but a change to any part sends the whole address as shown; QuickBooks then answers for the long part itself. The sandbox check of the field limits settles how often that can happen.

Left by step 4 (2026-11-16):

- **The Documenso webhook has only met the stub.** The local Documenso could not reach the portal's address; the worker asks about every open form every 5 minutes, so a missed webhook only delays "Signed".
- **The consent records no address locally**: no client-IP header reaches it on localhost. Behind the real proxy it records what `clientIpFrom()` reads.
- **A payment requirement paid more than 12 months ago reads "No invoice yet"**: stored figures keep paid invoices for 12 months.
- **The Item list's inactive items have only met the stub.** QuickBooks' plain `select * from Item` is understood to answer active items only, and a read replaces the stored list, so an item made inactive may drop out of it and the requirement page then name it by number. The sandbox check of `ITEM_QUERY` (checks table above) settles it.
- **The browser test of installability fails in a worktree with a long path** (Chromium's service-worker storage passes the Windows path limit). It passes in the main tree and in short-named worktrees.

Left by step 5 (2026-11-16):

- **After an email change that met a busy roster, the old address can still ask for a code** until the asked-for refresh runs (normally the worker's next wake). Sessions and unused codes end at once; the person row keeps the old address until the refresh.
- **Signed-form evidence is never re-verified on its own.** A hash mismatch is found only when evidence is fetched again. A complete request whose evidence was not yet fetched when a different company is connected is deleted with the others, and its evidence is never fetched (the document stays in Documenso; the audit row counts it). The webhook does not trigger the fetch; it waits for the worker's next wake. Past restarts of a form show only in the audit log.
- **The certificate and audit-log PDFs are kept but not compared** on a later fetch, as Documenso may render them on request; only the sealed PDFs and the originals are.
- **Making a customer active again is not offered**; the client's function takes an active flag. "Try again now" hands an item to the worker's next wake (within a minute), and the queue tries at most 10 items a wake.

Left by the review of step 5 (2026-11-16):

- **A moved student's signed form is the old family's to read.** It still counts for the student under the new family, which reads only "Signed": no signers, no download. The old family no longer sees it either (its student is gone); staff reach its evidence on the Forms page.
- **A form sealed in Documenso at the moment its student moved is left in Documenso, not kept by the portal.** The move had already reset the request, so the new family is asked to sign again; the worker never deletes it and audits `form.document.withdraw.kept` for staff.
- **The copy for a guardian whose student moved before the evidence was kept goes only as an attachment**: a form too large to attach is skipped, since the download link is now the new family's.
- **Any guardian of the family can check in another guardian's own place** ("Check everyone in"), as the spec's wording allows. Whether it should be the scanning guardian's place and students' alone is the owner's call. Checking in from inside the installed app was decided on 2026-11-16 (owner's decisions): nothing is built for it; the camera opens the code in the phone's browser, as the reminder and the sheet say, and native apps may bring link capture later (Later, if wanted).

Left by step 6 (2026-11-16):

- **The portal reaches Documenso at its public address, and Documenso the portal at `APP_URL`,** so the server must reach its own public names (`docs/runbooks/first-install.md` has the check). A separate internal address for Documenso's API would remove that; it is a change to the Forms settings and client.
- **Some parent journeys have not been walked by keyboard in a browser.** `tests/e2e/keyboard-journeys.spec.ts` (step 8, `dc2d5df`) walks journeys 1 to 8 of `docs/accessibility-checks.md` by keyboard alone, but can't reach agreeing to sign or Documenso's frame (the seed sets no forms up), saving the phone and address form (QuickBooks is never reached) or pay-instead. Documenso's unnamed button is in `docs/ui-backlog.md`.
- **A season's requirements are still a list of lines** on `/staff/seasons/[id]`, and the forms matrix does not use the season ruler as its header (`docs/ui-backlog.md`). The clean-up preview, a family's roster page, its progress and its volunteer credit are tables since step 8 (`2f2febd`, `00c60b9`, `b24765f`, `86c99f4`).

Left by the review of step 6 (2026-11-16):

- **The daily check now has a QuickBooks move's per-student buy-out invoice voided** through the queue (read live, voided only with nothing paid), as a portal move does; before, it was only flagged. The owner may want it the other way: then the flag's words must say the invoice is still open and now on the other family.
- **The pay-instead journey has not been walked in a browser** with a keyboard or a screen reader (`docs/accessibility-checks.md`, journey 9): the e2e run can't reach QuickBooks, so no buy-out is made there.

Left by the review of step 7 (2026-11-17):

- **A moved student's earlier invoices show on the new family's Payments page.** Invoices follow the customer they were raised on, so once a student moves (by staff or in QuickBooks) the new family sees what the old household was billed and paid on that student's record in the last 12 months, and any still open with Pay. `tests/integration/family-money.test.ts` asserts the open case as intended, and the spec says nothing either way. The owner's call: show a student's invoices only from the move onward (leaving older open ones to Reconciliation), or record that it is meant.
- **A signature through a stale signing link is stopped by starting the form again, not by checking the signer.** Blocking a person, or the reconcile closing their link, resets the family's unfinished forms still waiting for them and withdraws the documents (`formsForGoneSigner()`), so the request no longer points at a document they can sign. `readAndStore()` itself still counts any recipient of the document it is pointed at; a form the guardian had already signed before they lost access is kept.
- **The check-in sheet's typed address is long and random.** The sheet now says to ask the person running the shift, who can record the check-in, when a phone can't scan; a short code to type was not built (the owner's decision of 2026-11-16 left typed codes out).

Left by step 8 (2026-11-17):

- **A buy-out of a student moved in QuickBooks is followed less closely than a portal move's.** The refresh that reads the move runs the forms and volunteer follow-ups (`d47bbb7`); buy-outs stay with the daily check after the money run. So a paid per-student buy-out is not flagged `student_moved` for the treasurer; one still `being_invoiced` is caught only by the first check after it is invoiced, its invoice showing on the new family's money until then; a `being_voided` or stuck one is not flagged; and the void waits up to a day, for a money run that succeeded.
- **A move follow-up that keeps failing for one student stops every refresh after it** (`follow_up_failed`), as a reconcile that keeps failing does: the old roster stays and the Health page shows the failed run, but nothing gets past it on its own. `formsForMovedStudent()` resets a request even while an open holds it (`preparing_at`), as the portal move always has; a refresh can now reach that at any moment.
- **Payment reminders rarely say an amount.** Figures are fresh for 300 seconds (`money.settings`) and the worker reads every family once a day, so at 9:00 most payment reminders say "Not paid yet" and point to Payments. No staff page shows or changes the payment and checklist reminders: they follow the due date alone.
- **Some buy-outs still can't be closed.** A create given up after it may have reached QuickBooks (`sent`) can't be closed or ended: the portal can't tell whether the invoice exists. A void that is stuck or given up has no way out but "Try again now" while its item is still stuck. An invoice flagged as on another customer that the treasurer voids there stays flagged; the row says to move it back instead.
- **A failed read of the products and services after the daily run is only audited** (`money.items.read.failed`): neither the run's detail nor the Health page's money line says so. The volunteer reminders' Health line can't tell a wake before 9:00 from one with nothing due.
- **The audit-row test is a heuristic** (`tests/unit/audit-rows.test.ts`): it follows calls by name and can't prove a caller's audit row is in the same transaction as a helper's write. `scripts/restore.sh`'s own staging database (`<scratch>_restoring`) carries no run's mark, so one a cut-off restore left is listed by the teardown as left alone rather than dropped; `restore.sh` clears it at its next run.
- **A shift's page is still titled "Sign up for a shift"** (`docs/ui-backlog.md`); a form's page is named for its form since step 8.
- **A restored older backup holds the backup time recorded before it was dumped**, so starting a newer version after restoring it is stopped by the migrate step's backup check until a fresh backup is made (`docs/runbooks/restore.md` says so).
