# Booster Rocket v1 — Roadmap

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-booster-rocket-v1-design.md` (later specs in that folder override parts of it)
**Visual system:** `DESIGN.md`, `design/reference/`
**Rules for agents:** `AGENTS.md`

This page is the status of the build. Update it in the same commit that finishes a step.

## Decisions from the owner (2026-11-14)

1. **Scope is the whole v1 spec.** Nothing is cut for a first release.
2. **Forms are signed in Documenso from the start**, as the spec describes.
3. **Parents edit their phone and address.** The portal writes to QuickBooks; the read-only rule is lifted for the write paths named below.
4. **A parent stays signed in for 90 days, renewed by use.** Every request still re-checks QuickBooks links and blocks.
5. **The portal can be installed as an app** on iOS and Android, as well as used as a website. This was not in the v1 spec.
6. **The portal's address is set by whoever installs it.** No hostname is written into the code, and nothing depends on one hosting provider.
7. **Work is committed locally on the branch `parent-portal`.** Nothing is pushed or deployed until the owner says so.

Facts from the owner: Intuit's production app assessment has not been started. The real books have Family, Student and Parent customer types; not every parent has their own email address. Invoices are sent with online ACH payment on.

## Decisions from the owner (2026-11-16)

8. **Checking in from inside the installed app is a lower priority, and nothing is built for it now**: no code to type in and no scanner inside the app. Scanning the shift's QR code with the phone's camera stays the way to check in. On an iPhone that opens Safari, not the installed app, because Apple lets only native apps capture a link; a volunteer whose Safari has no session signs in there once and is brought back to the check-in page. The owner may later move from the app installed from the browser to brandable native iOS and Android apps, which would bring link capture with them (Later, if wanted, below).

## How the work is done

One slice at a time, each small enough to see on a screen, with a short brief instead of a long plan. Tests are written with the code. The privacy gates are automatic (`AGENTS.md`). A security review is added where a slice writes to QuickBooks, changes sign-in, shows money or stores new personal data. Each step ends with the full suite green and a local commit. Slices in the same step are built in parallel in separate worktrees and merged at the end of the step.

## Known gaps

Left by the review of step 2 (2026-11-15):

- **No credit line on a parent's page for a family with other customers under it.** QuickBooks' `BalanceWithJobs` counts every customer under the family, so the parent's credit line is shown only when the roster holds nothing else there. Since step 4 the worker's daily read compares each customer's own balance with its invoices for every family, and Balances lists the ones that differ.
- **Payments and Home ask the roster first; `loadFamilyMoney()` alone does not.** It refuses an inactive family and a row that isn't the family's, but not a roster cache from another company than the connected one, or a broken shape: a new page that shows money must call `loadMyFamily()` first (`AGENTS.md`, Money).
- **Figures go "not current" when the portal is only busy.** When every place for a live read is taken for 2 seconds, or another copy of the portal holds the family's lock or the connection row, the family sees its last figures with Pay switched off for up to 30 seconds. The note no longer blames an outage; waiting for the read under way would be better.
- **A code step left on a shared device** is still picked up by the next visitor for its 10 minutes: they see "we've sent a code" and can ask for it to be sent again, until they choose "Use a different email", which now hands them nothing of the earlier person.

Left by step 3 (2026-11-15):

- **A phone saved while the roster lock is held shows the old phone in the details box for a few minutes.** The save no longer writes the cache under a refresh, or under a staff change that holds the lock: it asks the worker for another read (step 7, `890e0b9`), which brings the new phone in at its next wake, and the parent is told the Family page will catch up in a few minutes. QuickBooks is right throughout, and the edit page reads it live.
- **The field limits for a parent's phone and address are cautious guesses** (phone 30; address lines 200; city, region and country 100; postal code 20): Intuit's reference could not be read when they were chosen. The sandbox check above settles them.
- **Coverage is everyone or a chosen list.** The spec's filter by grade or section has no data behind it (Waiting on the owner). A due date sends reminders for every kind: forms on their schedule, volunteering, and payments and checklist items 14 and 3 days before (step 8, `17968b1`), with no schedule of their own for the last two. There is no way to delete a whole season (staff stop showing it and remove its steps). Requirements are reordered within a step since step 7 (`a82ee6b`).
- **Opening the audit log is not audited**, even filtered to one person's record; the export is. Say if a filtered view should be.
- **The backup test needs Postgres's own tools on the CI runner.** Closed by step 7: CI names the Postgres service's container in `BR_TEST_DB_CONTAINER`, so the test runs `pg_dump`, `pg_restore` and `psql` inside it, the server's own version, as `deploy/` does. A runner's own client could be older than the server, or new enough to write settings Postgres 18 refuses. Not yet seen on GitHub (checks table).
- **A header the proxy doesn't send still puts the staff door in one shared bucket.** With `CLIENT_IP_HEADER=none` Better Auth is told there is no address and its limiter stands aside, and the portal's own per-IP sign-in limits are skipped whenever the address is unknown. But when a header is named and never arrives, Better Auth (which can't be told per request) counts every visitor together: three staff sign-in requests in ten seconds for the whole club. The Health page's parent line says so once the latest sign-ins have no address; the fix is the right header.
- **An untouched address part that is too long is sent again when the parent edits another part.** A phone-only save never sends the address, and only changed fields are checked, but a change to any part sends the whole address as shown; QuickBooks then answers for the long part itself. The sandbox check of the field limits settles how often that can happen.

Left by step 4 (2026-11-16):

- **The Documenso webhook has only met the stub.** The local Documenso could not reach the portal's address; the worker asks about every open form every 5 minutes, so a missed webhook only delays "Signed".
- **The consent records no address locally**: no client-IP header reaches it on localhost. Behind the real proxy it records what `clientIpFrom()` reads.
- **A payment requirement paid more than 12 months ago reads "No invoice yet"**: stored figures keep paid invoices for 12 months.
- **The Item list's inactive items have only met the stub.** QuickBooks' plain `select * from Item` is understood to answer active items only, and a read replaces the stored list, so an item made inactive may drop out of it and the requirement page then name it by number. The sandbox check of `ITEM_QUERY` (checks table above) settles it.
- **The browser test of installability fails in a worktree with a long path** (Chromium's service-worker storage passes the Windows path limit). It passes in the main tree and in short-named worktrees.

Left by step 5 (2026-11-16):

- **After an email change that met a busy roster, the old address can still ask for a code** until the asked-for refresh runs (normally the worker's next wake). Sessions and unused codes end at once; the person row keeps the old address until the refresh.
- **Signed-form evidence is never re-verified on its own.** A hash mismatch is found only when evidence is fetched again. A complete request whose evidence was not yet fetched when a different company is connected is deleted with the others, and its evidence is never fetched (the document stays in Documenso; the audit row counts it). The webhook does not trigger the fetch; it waits for the worker's next wake. Past restarts of a form show only in the audit log.
- **The certificate and audit-log PDFs are kept but not compared** on a later fetch, as Documenso may render them on request; only the sealed PDFs and the originals are.
- **Making a customer active again is not offered**; the client's function takes an active flag. "Try again now" hands an item to the worker's next wake (within a minute), and the queue tries at most 10 items a wake.

Left by the review of step 5 (2026-11-16):

Left by the review of step 7 (2026-11-17):

- **A moved student's earlier invoices show on the new family's Payments page.** Invoices follow the customer they were raised on, so once a student moves (by staff or in QuickBooks) the new family sees what the old household was billed and paid on that student's record in the last 12 months, and any still open with Pay. `tests/integration/family-money.test.ts` asserts the open case as intended, and the spec says nothing either way. Left by step 8 (2026-11-17):

- **No longer true since stage 3's slice 9c, which follows no move made in QuickBooks at all** (no follow-up and no void: the daily check flags such a per-student buy-out once, `student_moved`, for Reconciliation). It read: **A buy-out of a student moved in QuickBooks is followed less closely than a portal move's.** The refresh that reads the move runs the forms and volunteer follow-ups (`d47bbb7`); buy-outs stay with the daily check after the money run. - **A restored older backup holds the backup time recorded before it was dumped**, so starting a newer version after restoring it is stopped by the migrate step's backup check until a fresh backup is made (`docs/runbooks/restore.md` says so).
