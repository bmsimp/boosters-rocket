no test, or a test that covers only part. **Missing** means no code. **Changed** and **Dropped** mean a later spec or the
owner deliberately changed or removed it; the row names the source. Paths are from the repository root; a test is named
by its file and the start of its title. "Owner check" means only a real company, phone or host can settle it (the
roadmap's checks table). For every request the portal sends QuickBooks, that owner check is now one page: Settings,
QuickBooks, **Check the portal's requests** (`/staff/settings/quickbooks/check`, `src/lib/quickbooks/sandbox-check/`,
`tests/integration/quickbooks-check.test.ts`), run by an Admin against an Intuit sandbox company.

## §6 Roster management

### §6.1 Staff operations

For a linked family, making someone inactive (and a parent's changes) go to QuickBooks first, and the cache and the app's own record follow QuickBooks' answer (`src/lib/roster/writes.ts`, under the roster lock). Since stage 3's slice 9g a linked student's or member's make-inactive goes by the record (`deactivateLinked()`, by the app's id, no version from the page), sent with its link's own version, a stale answer settled by the Active flag, and the family action by the family record's app id and a stamp of whom its page covered (`coverStamp()`), each step with its own link's version (`inactive-balance-rule.test.ts`). Since stage 3's slice 9e a rename of a linked family, member or student and a student added to a linked family are saved in the portal first and then sent, a rename with its link's own version (`accounting_link.synced_token`), a stale answer settled by the field and what QuickBooks refuses kept as a `refused` item on Reconciliation (`renameLinked()`, `addStudentToLinked()`, `sendPortalValue()`; `linked-saved-first.test.ts`). Since stage 3's slice 9f a student's move is saved in the portal first too, its follow-ups run once at the save by the app's ids, then sent for a linked student with its link's own version, a stale answer settled by the field (the parent QuickBooks has the student under against the family moved from), and kept refused into a family with no customer (`moveStudent()`; `moves-saved-first.test.ts`). Since stage 3's slice 9d a new family is an exception too: it is saved in the portal first and sent only by staff who may link families to accounting, and every change to a family with no link (or a student with none) is saved in the portal only (`addFamily()`, `changeInPortal()`, `createInQuickBooksAndLink()`; `families-by-hand.test.ts`). Since stage 3's slice 9c of the app-owned families spec each write changes its own record itself (`changeOwnRecord()`: a new family, member or student's record and its link, `created` by the staff member who asked; a name; an inactive flag; a move, saved first since slice 9f, none; a Parent customer none), audited as `appRecord` on the write's row, and one QuickBooks took that the portal couldn't take in waits on the queue marked sent, so the found-done path makes the same change: `roster-writes.test.ts` "each write QuickBooks took changes the app's own record too", `roster-queue.test.ts` "a family staff added that the portal couldn't take in" and "a move QuickBooks took that the portal couldn't take in". Nothing made, renamed, moved or made inactive in QuickBooks changes a record (`app-records.test.ts` "the records stand alone").
