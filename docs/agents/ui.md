# UI, in depth

What this covers: the components pages are built from, the tokens, the sections of `booster.css`, the gallery, and the installed app, beside the UI rules in `AGENTS.md`. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

The look is **Material Design 3, in its Expressive style** (the owner's choice of 2026-11-26, `docs/agents/owner-taste.md` rule 7; it replaces the calm, Linear-like look of 2026-11-21), under the brand kit (`brand/BRAND.md`): M3 for components, layout, interaction, states, motion and accessibility, with bolder type, more color, rounder shapes and spring motion, but never at the cost of readability. Inter stays, self-hosted (never Google Fonts). The club's color seeds M3's color scheme, so every club gets its own palette; the neutrals and the status colors are fixed. The club leads; the product shows only as the "Powered by Boosters Rocket" badge. Structure comes from tone (a filled card on the page's gray ground), spacing and the type scale, not hairlines; only things that float have a shadow.

The owner's decisions of 2026-11-26, which the code follows:
- **D1, neutrals:** the gray surfaces and neutrals are fixed, the same for every club: no club tint.
- **D2, club color:** the primary family keeps the club's own chroma at M3's fixed tones, so contrast is guaranteed by tone; the club's exact color appears only on the logo tile and the app icon. Secondary is the hue at low chroma (16), tertiary the hue plus about 60 (chroma 24), neither more colorful than the seed.
- **D3, color library:** `@material/material-color-utilities` (Apache-2.0, pinned at 0.4.0) makes the scheme on the server (`src/lib/theme/scheme.ts`; the Club page's live preview runs it in the browser too), light, dark and both high-contrast modes from the one seed.
- **D4, buttons:** in rows, forms and toolbars a 40px button inside a 48px touch target; a page's main button and sheet and dialog footers 56px. The set: filled, tonal, outlined, text, and danger (outlined, or filled inside its ConfirmDialog), fully round, the corner squaring off a little on press with a spring.
- **D5, staff navigation:** an expanded navigation rail from 1200px wide, always open, every group's pages under its heading, no collapse. Below 1200px the staff tab bar and the group hubs. Settings is list-detail: its list pane beside the page.
- **D6, parent navigation:** top links from 840px, on M3's pill; the phone keeps M3's navigation bar, labels always shown.
- **D7:** "nothing is a pill" is retired. Status labels (StatusBadge, StateWords' badges) have 8px corners, so they never look like buttons.
- **D8, focus ring:** 3px in the club's primary, offset 2, held to 3:1 against every surface it can sit on (`tests/unit/design-tokens.test.ts`); where a club's primary can't hold it, that mode's ring is on-surface.

Rules:
- No fold-outs, the owner's rule of 2026-11-25. Nothing in the app is a `<details>`/`<summary>`, and there is no disclosure primitive: a form a person opens is a `Drawer`, a press that needs a second look a `ConfirmDialog`, an action on many rows the `DataTable` selection, two ways to do a task a `StepsChoice`, and an explanation one or two sentences in the open, or nothing. An earlier version of something (a message's) is a list of links, each to its own view. `tests/unit/no-fold-outs.test.ts` refuses `<details`, `<summary` or `HowThisWorks` anywhere under `src/app`, with no exceptions.
- Words on buttons and by them (the owner's of 2026-11-25): a button's label is a verb and its object, saying exactly what happens ("Disconnect QuickBooks", "Replace this portal's key", "Change the portal id"), never a bare "Disconnect", "More" or "Details". A consequence a person must know before pressing is one short plain sentence by the button ("Other portals on this QuickBooks company through Boosters Rocket may be disconnected too."), and the same in its ConfirmDialog. An explanation is one or two sentences, saying only what someone needs to act; keep every safety fact, said once and short, and leave the rest out of the app. The voice is `PRODUCT.md`'s.
- Dialogs (`Sheet`, under `Drawer` and `ConfirmDialog`, `src/app/components/ui/sheet.tsx`): the native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`, so the page behind is inert and Esc closes it; it is named by its heading (`aria-labelledby`), the focus goes in (a ConfirmDialog's Cancel first, or the first element with `data-autofocus`) and comes back to the button that opened it, and the page doesn't scroll while it is open (`html.sheet-open`). A Drawer fills a phone's screen and slides in from the right from 600px, springing in; a ConfirmDialog is small and centered. A Drawer is opened by its address: its button is a link to the page's own address with `?param=value`, pushed without a load, so Back closes it, a refresh opens it again, and without JavaScript the link loads the page with the form drawn in it (an open `<dialog>` in the page). Its contents are drawn with the page, closed, so a test of the page's markup reads them.
- Bulk select: what a bulk action sends comes from the browser (ids, or "all that match" with the ids left out). The server checks every id again against what the person may change, and works out "all that match" from its own query, never from a count it was sent. Its Drawer says first what would happen to each row, then what did, row by row, with its press and Cancel (later "Do the next N" and Done) along the sheet's foot at 56px, as ConfirmDialog's are. Each row is done by the row's own press, in its own transaction with that press's audit row, so one refused never undoes another; at most `PRESS_MAX` (200) a press, the rest handed back untried (`eachRow()`, `idsFrom()`, `bulkWords()` in `src/lib/bulk.ts`). Where every row that matches is on the page (Accounting links, Reconciliation), the header box ticks them all and no "Select all M that match" is drawn.
- Actions are buttons. anything that starts or changes something, or takes the person to the next step of a task, is a button, the owner's rule of 2026-11-25: `Button` for a form's submit, `LinkButton` for going to an action or a step, primary for the one thing the page or step is for and secondary for the others. A plain underlined link is only for reading more, help, going back to where the person came from (breadcrumbs, "Back to …"), or a record's own page from a list or table. A link alone on its line still takes `.link-line`. `tests/unit/actions-as-buttons.test.ts` refuses a bare `<a>` or `<Link>` on a staff, parent or sign-in page whose written words start like an action or a step ("Next:", "Skip to", "Connect" and so on); words from a constant aren't seen, so it is a floor. The gallery's Button section shows the rule's do and don't.
- No new one-off class where a primitive does the job. A page block that needs one goes in its area's block of `booster.css` (below), built from tokens.
- No hex color, rgb or named color in a component: tokens only. A fixed color the stylesheet gains is added to `src/lib/theme/palette.ts` in the same commit, where `tests/unit/design-tokens.test.ts` checks it matches the stylesheet and passes its contrast in every mode.
- Font sizes in rem, from the scale, never under 0.8125rem (`--text-xs`); body text is 1rem. Figures are tabular only where they are read as figures: `.num` (and `numeric` on a DataTable column, `KeyValue`'s `numeric`), amounts, stats, a table's times; the body keeps Inter's proportional figures, whose tabular hyphen is set wide.
- Every touch target is at least 48 by 48 (M3's minimum, `--target`): buttons (drawn 40px inside 48, by a clear 4px edge above and below their fill, so the element's own box is the target), fields (56px), tick boxes and radios, tabs, rail and menu links, `.link-line`, a table's links. `size="sm"` is narrower, not shorter. `tests/unit/design-tokens.test.ts` refuses `44px` anywhere in the stylesheet, and the e2e checks measure 48.
- Every status is words plus an icon; color only repeats them. The status colors are the brand's fixed set (done/paid a tick, due a clock, late/overdue an alert, info an i) plus the neutral gray, as M3 custom color groups (a color and its container), never harmonized to the club; a club never changes them. A green club's primary sits near "done" and a red club's near "late": the words and icons carry the state. The current tab, rail item and settings item sit on the secondary container's pill and are bold, so color never carries "current" alone.
- Fully round is for buttons, the tab and rail pill, the floating toolbar, avatars and dots. Status labels are 8px (D7); cards 20px; dialogs 28px; side sheets 16px on their leading edge; fields 4px; the logo tile 12px.
- Only things that float have a shadow (`--shadow-3`: the floating toolbar, dialogs, side sheets); cards are flat and parted from the page by tone. Tables inside a `Card` run to its edges by themselves (`--box-pad`); a list inside a card is a group of tiles on the card.
- Motion: springs (`--spring-fast`, `--spring-default`, CSS `linear()` curves) for things that move (a button's corner on press, sheets and the dialog arriving, the tab pill, the floating toolbar); color and opacity on M3's standard easing (`--effects`, 150ms), never bouncing. Text never moves while it is read. Reduced motion (the device's or `data-motion="reduce"`): nothing moves at all.
- State layers: hover washes the content color over the container at 8%, press at 10% (`--sl-hover`, `--sl-press`), mixed into the fill with `color-mix()` so the result is a solid color whose contrast can be measured.

## Tokens

Section 1 of `booster.css`; the fixed colors are mirrored in `src/lib/theme/palette.ts` (ratios for every pair and mode are in the gallery's Contrast section, and the seed picker there shows any club's scheme).

- Fixed color roles (D1, the same for every club), M3's names: `--ground` (the page: surface-container in light, surface in dark), `--surface` (the bars), `--card` (a card's fill: the lowest container in light, the container in dark; the staff pane's cards take the low container), `--surface-container-lowest`, `-low` (side sheets), `--surface-container`, `-high` (dialogs), `-highest` (table headers, the connected group), `--on-surface`, `--on-surface-variant` (secondary text: 4.5:1 on every surface), `--outline` (a field's or tick box's edge, drawn marks: 3:1), `--outline-variant` (hairlines, an outlined button's edge), and the status groups `--done`, `--due`, `--late` (also M3's error, with `--on-late` for a filled danger button), `--info`, each with its `--*-container`, plus `--neutral-container`. `--badge-ink` is the "Powered by" badge's one ink; `--br-*` (the product's indigo, flame and the mark's fills, and `--br-glow`) draw only the sign-in page's full-color badge and the setup pages' product icon. `--scrim` is behind a dialog; `--card-edge` is transparent, and the outline in high contrast.
- Club roles (D2, runtime: `clubScheme()` in `src/lib/theme/scheme.ts`, written on `<html>` by `src/app/layout.tsx` as `--<role>-l`, `-d`, `-lhc`, `-dhc`, and picked per mode by the token blocks; a club that hasn't chosen a color gets the brand's Launch Indigo, `DEFAULT_COLOR`): `--primary`, `--on-primary`, `--primary-container`, `--on-primary-container`, and the same for `--secondary` and `--tertiary`; `--focus`, the ring (D8); `--seed` and `--on-seed`, the club's exact color and its ink, for the logo tile (`.monogram`) alone. Tones: light 40/100/90/10, dark 80/20/30/90, high contrast light 25/100/90/0 and dark 90/10/25/100 (color, on-color, container, on-container). Uses: primary for filled buttons, links, the ring, a ticked box, the current step; the primary container for the tonal card and the floating toolbar; the secondary container for the current tab, rail and settings item, a ticked row, tonal buttons and a chosen option.
- Display modes: light and dark (`data-theme` or the device), high contrast for each (`data-contrast="more"`: M3's high contrast level, 7:1 text, lines in full ink, cards edged), text size Large and Larger (`data-text`), reduced motion (`data-motion` or the device: nothing moves), and forced colors (section 6). Any element can carry `data-theme`/`data-contrast` with `.theme-sample` to show a mode in place.
- Type: `--font` (Inter through `next/font`, `--font-sans`; never Google Fonts in the app). M3's roles as `font` shorthands, emphasized: `--display-s` (a staff page's title from 840px, 2.25rem), `--headline-l` (a page title, 2rem), `--headline-m`, `--headline-s` (a sheet's or dialog's title), `--title-l` (a card's title, the club's name), `--title-m`, `--title-s` (table headers, section labels, rail headings), `--body-l` (body, 1rem, everywhere), `--body-m` (secondary lines only), `--label-l` (buttons, nav), `--label-m` (status labels, tab labels: M3's 12px raised to 0.8125rem). Size steps for page blocks: `--text-xs` 0.8125rem (the floor), `--text-sm` 0.875rem, `--text-control` 0.875rem, `--text-md` 1rem, `--text-lg` 1.375rem, `--text-xl` 2rem, `--text-2xl` 2.25rem. M3's smaller label and body sizes (12 and 11px) are raised to 0.8125rem; nothing is ever under it.
- Space: `--s1` 4px … `--s7` 48px on M3's 4px grid; `--cell`, the page gutter, 16px compact and 24px from 600px. Breakpoints are M3's window classes: 600 (medium), 840 (expanded: the parent's top links, two columns, tables unstacked, the settings list pane), 1200 (large: the staff rail).
- Shape: `--shape-xs` 4, `-s` 8, `-m` 12, `-l` 16, `-l-inc` 20, `-xl` 28, `-xl-inc` 32, `-xxl` 48, `-full`. Elevation: `--shadow-3`. Motion: `--spring-fast`/`-dur`, `--spring-default`/`-dur`, `--effects`/`-dur`. State layers: `--sl-hover` 8%, `--sl-press` 10%. `--target` 48px; `--ring` 3px.

## Installed app

- The manifest (`src/app/manifest.ts`) and the icons are built on each request from the club's settings and hold no hostname; the manifest's theme color is the light surface and its background (the splash) the light ground, not the club's color, which is on the icon. Icon names are `ICONS` in `src/lib/pwa/icons.ts`. `InstallHint` and `RefreshOnResume` (`src/app/components/pwa/`) are given no club or family data.
- The page's `theme-color` is the top bar's surface: `#f7f9fd` in light, `#111316` in dark (`THEME_COLOR`, `src/lib/theme/palette.ts`), by `prefers-color-scheme`.
- `public/offline.html` is drawn in the same look with its own inline styles and the system font, the same for every club. It carries no product mark or name: `tests/unit/pwa-static.test.ts` refuses "Booster" in it, since the product's name is also the default club's name before setup.
