# Volunteering

What this covers: events, shifts, sign-ups, check-in and credit. Buy-outs are in `docs/agents/buyouts.md`. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

## Volunteering

- Events, shifts and sign-ups (`src/lib/volunteer/`; staff `Volunteer.Event.Read` and `Volunteer.Event.ReadWrite`). Since stage 2 of the app-owned families spec, sign-ups (`volunteer_signup`), credit (`volunteer_credit`) and reminder claims (`volunteer_reminder`) name the app's own family (`family_id`, never null) and student (`student_id`), each a `restrict` foreign key, with checks that a student's place names its student and a check-in none (`volunteer_signup_filler_record`, `volunteer_credit_source_record`); no QuickBooks id. Migration `0026_volunteer_app_ids_additive` filled them through `accounting_link` by the kind each column holds, in the roster's company, and stops on any row with no record of that kind; `0027_volunteer_app_ids_finish` rewrote each due-date claim's key to the app's family (`nudgeKey()`: `<family>|<due date>|<guardian>`), so no reminder goes twice, and dropped the QuickBooks columns (`tests/integration/migrate-volunteer.test.ts`). In the code they are `AppFamilyId`, `AppStudentId` and `SubjectId` (`Filler`, `Slot`, `CreditRow`). New audit rows name the app's ids (`familyId`, `studentId` in the body; `volunteer.credit.family.read` and `volunteer.signup.moved` target the record). Staff pages and the worker take the families from `loadSeasonFamilies()` (`docs/agents/seasons.md`); a family's credit page is addressed by the app's family (stage 3's third slice; `familyRecordAt()` first), found as the season keeps it in one place (`seasonFamilyAt()`), the credit list links each family by it (`CreditFamilyRow.id`), and its outside-credit form sends the family's and students' app ids (uuids, `NOT_FROM_PAGE` otherwise). An event's date and a shift's times are the club's wall-clock time, read as instants in the club's zone only when needed (`clock.ts`); an end at or before the start is the next morning. The cancelling cut-off is the setting `volunteer.settings` (default 48 hours before the shift).
- A parent sees a shift's count of places filled and their own family's sign-ups, never who else filled a place: not on a page, in a prop, an email or a calendar file. Parent reads and writes go through `src/lib/volunteer/my-volunteer.ts` (and `check-in.ts`, and `loadMyCredit()` in `credit.ts`), which read the session's family (`ctx.familyId`) and its own active students as the app's records (`activeFamilyOf()`, `sessionFamily()` in `src/lib/families/session-family.ts`; no link, and nothing of a family whose record is inactive) and read and write by those records alone; no action takes a student list from the page any more; a place is filled by the guardian ("me") or an active student of that family, sent by the page as the student's app id (never a QuickBooks id) and checked again in the action, under the roster lock. `src/lib/volunteer/setup.ts` (sign-up lists by name) is staff only.
- Signing up is rate-limited per guardian (`volunteer_signup_parent`); it locks the shift row, counts, then inserts, holding the roster lock shared, so the last place goes to one person and no change of company runs in between; one person can't hold places on two shifts whose times overlap, and a roster refresh holding the lock is answered in words. A sign-up belongs to the family it was made from, and only while its student is still active and directly under that family (the student's own record, active and in that family: `studentsStillIn()`): a student who moves takes their places out of the old family's lists and standings, and the new family doesn't count them. Emails (with an `.ics` attachment, by SMTP or Graph; a cancellation's file is `METHOD:CANCEL`) go to the guardian who took the place, about their own sign-up only, and only while that guardian's link to the family is open (their guardian row in the place's family, by `guardian.family_id`) and they aren't blocked. Staff changes email only about shifts still to come; cancelling an event or a shift asks first; a shift or event that is over can't be cancelled, and a volunteer requirement with live places counting toward it can't be removed (`src/lib/volunteer/removal.ts`). Tests: `tests/helpers/volunteer.ts` (`seedEvent()`, `seedShift()`, `seedSignup()`, `seedCheckIn()`, `seedOutsideCredit()`, `shiftAround()`; the seeds take families and students by QuickBooks id and store the app's records, `familyRecord()` and `studentRecord()`, bringing the records into step first when one has none yet, so a test that seeds one seeds a roster company first).
- Check-in: each shift has its own random code (`volunteer_shift.checkin_code`, never its id; staff can make a new one), drawn as a QR code on a printable sheet (`/staff/volunteer/check-in/[shiftId]`, `qrcode` 1.5.4's matrix as SVG in a server component). It opens the parent page `/volunteer/check-in/[code]`, which checks in only the session family's own live sign-ups, from 30 minutes before the start until the end in the club's zone, checked again in the action; one live credit per sign-up (a unique index); a sign-up whose check-in staff removed can't be checked in again by a parent (staff add it back); rate-limited (`volunteer_checkin_parent`).
- Credit (`volunteer_credit`): a check-in, a check-in staff added, or volunteering outside the portal toward one requirement, its minutes and shifts fixed when made. `allocateCredit()` (`src/lib/volunteer/allocation.ts`, pure) spends it by spec §11.4: required before optional, per student before per family, then due date, then season order; each requirement takes first the credits later ones could use least, then in earned order; shifts take whole credits, hours split minutes; each credit is spent once. A waived requirement takes no credit (`allocateFamilies()` leaves it out). A place on a shift that has ended without a check-in is no longer "signed up for" (`signedUpAmounts()`). Staff corrections take `Volunteer.Credit.ReadWrite` (`/staff/volunteer/credit`; a family's page is audited before it shows anything); a removed credit keeps its row with a staff-only reason. When a student moves, credit stays with the family it was earned for.
