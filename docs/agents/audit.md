# Audit

What this covers: the audit page's record filter. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

## The record filter

- Audit: the audit page's record filter (`src/lib/audit/query.ts`) matches the target exactly, except for an app family or student (`family:<id>`, `student:<id>`, or the id alone): it also finds the rows that named that record's QuickBooks customers (its links in the roster's company, and since stage 4's slice M3b its links an unlink ended, `accounting_link_ended`, each customer only inside its window: rows written after the latest unlink of another record of the same kind (a family's or a student's, never a guardian row's) from that customer that came before this record's own (any, for its live link: no two records of a kind are linked to one customer at once), and at or before this record's own unlink of it, every bound an `ended_at` from the database's own clock (`statement_timestamp()` in the unlink, after its locks), the clock `audit_log.at` is written by, an open bound where there is none; so with no ended link anywhere it finds what it found before, and a row naming a customer while family A was linked to it is A's, never that of a family B linked to it after A's unlink: `customersOfRecord()`, `named()`; an `accounting.link.remove` row is found under the family by its target and under each student whose link it ended by `studentLinksEnded`), and the ticks' and waivers' rows keyed `<requirement>:<customer>` or `<requirement>:<id>`, so one search shows the rows written before an area moved to the app's ids and those written since (open question 17; old rows are never rewritten; `tests/integration/audit-record-filter.test.ts`). A customer's number is matched only under a target type that names a customer of the record's kind (`CUSTOMER_TARGET_TYPES`: `qbo_customer` and `family` for a family, `qbo_customer` and `student` for a student), never a job run's, a product's or a check's that happens to share it. A student's move is found under the student by its rows' target and under both its families, the family left and the family joined, by the records and customers its rows name in their body (`moveRowsOf()`: `roster.record.moved`, `roster.student.move.followed`, older ones by the families' customers, and `roster.student.move`; since stage 4's slice M4a, a move taken from the books on Accounting links, `accounting.difference.take` with `after.field` `place`, by the same keys as `roster.record.moved`; a take of a name, of inactive or, since slice M4b, of active is found by its target, the family or the student; so is `roster.family.signin_released`, which targets the family whose held sign-ins staff let through; since slice M5a, a student's family sent to the books from a difference, `accounting.difference.send` with `after.field` `place`, by the family the student is in (`after.familyId`) and the family record the books had them under, where one is (`after.fromFamilyId`); a send of a name or of inactive is found by its target, the family or the student, one row per press that reaches its try: a name's and a move's written in the transaction that queues the item (`before` the books' value the row showed, `after` with `queueId` and the kept items it `replaced`), a make-inactive's after its try with its `outcome` (`sent`, `inactive_there`, `stopped` with its `code`, `stale_again`, `queued`, `refused`), beside the item's own rows, written as for any write), as a guardian's and a buy-out's rows are found by theirs. A confirm on the accounting links screen (`accounting.link.confirm`, targeting the family) is found under each student it linked by the student ids its body names (`after.studentsLinked`, `confirmRowsOf()`).
