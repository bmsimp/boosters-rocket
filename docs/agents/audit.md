# Audit

What this covers: the audit page's record filter. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

## The record filter

- Audit: the audit page's record filter (`src/lib/audit/query.ts`) matches the target exactly, except for an app family or student (`family:<id>`, `student:<id>`, or the id alone): it also finds the rows that named that record's QuickBooks customers (its links of every company it was linked in, since stage 4's slice M8a, and since slice M3b its links an unlink ended, `accounting_link_ended`, each customer only inside its window: rows written after the latest unlink of another record of the same kind (a family's or a student's, never a guardian row's) from that customer in the same company that came before this record's own (any, for its live link: no two records of a kind are linked to one customer at once), and at or before this record's own unlink of it, every bound an `ended_at` from the database's own clock (`statement_timestamp()` in the unlink, after its locks), the clock `audit_log.at` is written by, an open bound where there is none, and each window cut to its company's time as the roster's (below); so with no ended link anywhere and no change of company it finds what it found before, and a row naming a customer while family A was linked to it is A's, never that of a family B linked to it after A's unlink: `customersOfRecord()`, `named()`; an `accounting.link.remove` row is found under the family by its target and under each student whose link it ended by `studentLinksEnded`), and the ticks' and waivers' rows keyed `<requirement>:<customer>` or `<requirement>:<id>`, so one search shows the rows written before an area moved to the app's ids and those written since (open question 17; old rows are never rewritten; `tests/integration/audit-record-filter.test.ts`). A customer's number is matched only under a target type that names a customer of the record's kind (`CUSTOMER_TARGET_TYPES`: `qbo_customer` and `family` for a family, `qbo_customer` and `student` for a student), never a job run's, a product's or a check's that happens to share it. A student's move is found under the student by its rows' target and under both its families, the family left and the family joined, by the records and customers its rows name in their body (`moveRowsOf()`: `roster.record.moved`, `roster.student.move.followed`, older ones by the families' customers, and `roster.student.move`; since stage 4's slice M4a, a move taken from the books on Accounting links, `accounting.difference.take` with `after.field` `place`, by the same keys as `roster.record.moved`; a take of a name, of inactive or, since slice M4b, of active is found by its target, the family or the student; so is `roster.family.signin_released`, which targets the family whose held sign-ins staff let through; since slice M5a, a student's family sent to the books from a difference, `accounting.difference.send` with `after.field` `place`, by the family the student is in (`after.familyId`) and the family record the books had them under, where one is (`after.fromFamilyId`); a send of a name or of inactive is found by its target, the family or the student, one row per press that reaches its try: a name's and a move's written in the transaction that queues the item (`before` the books' value the row showed, `after` with `queueId` and the kept items it `replaced`), a make-inactive's after its try with its `outcome` (`sent`, `inactive_there`, `stopped` with its `code`, `stale_again`, `queued`, `refused`), beside the item's own rows, written as for any write; since slice M5b, a layered family made inactive with everyone under it from its difference is found by its target, the family, one row after the family action's own `roster.family.deactivate` row (targeting the family's customer), `after` with whom it `covered` and its `outcome` (`sent`, `inactive_there`, `stopped`, `queued`), none when it was refused before anything was read; and Send the app's for all writes one `accounting.difference.send` row per difference it queues, each with `after.bulk` (the press's id) and found as a single send's is (a name by its target, a place also under both families by `after.familyId` and `after.fromFamilyId`), and one `accounting.difference.send_all` row, `targetType` `accounting_difference` with the press's id as its target, found by that target), as a guardian's and a buy-out's rows are found by theirs. A confirm on the accounting links screen (`accounting.link.confirm`, targeting the family) is found under each student it linked by the student ids its body names (`after.studentsLinked`, `confirmRowsOf()`). A company's time as the roster's (stage 4's slice M8a, `companyPeriods()`) is read from the change of company's own records: the `roster.cleared` rows whose `after.reason` is `different_company`, in id order, each ending a period of its `before.realmId` and starting one of its `after.realmId` at the row's `at`, which is the clear's transaction start, so the clear's own audit rows fall in the earlier company's period; the first company's first period is open below and the roster's company's current one open above, and A to B to A gives A two periods and B one. A live link belongs to the roster's company's current period, and an ended link to the latest period of its company that began before it ended (a `company_changed` link's, the one its clear ended, as its `ended_at` comes later in the clear's transaction); its window's `from` is the later of the bound above and the period's start, its `to` the earlier of its own end and the period's end. So a customer's number is never matched outside its company's time: customer 10 of company A is never customer 10 of company B, a kept family finds its earlier company's rows by that company's numbers after a switch, and a record linked in A only after a return from B finds nothing of A's first period. Where the records can't place a company the filter finds less, never more: a link of a company no `roster.cleared` row names and that isn't the roster's (or a live link of such a company) finds nothing by its number; a `roster.cleared` row without both realm ids as text ends the period before it and starts none; one whose `before.realmId` isn't the company the row before it started ends no period; and when the last row's `after.realmId` isn't the roster's company, the roster's company has no current period. A row written by another transaction that overlaps the clear's commit is placed by its own `at`, which is that transaction's start: one that began after the clear's transaction did is in the new company's time, whichever company's number it names (in the roster refresh's backstop, the clear's transaction is the refresh's, so its `at` comes before the refresh read QuickBooks).
