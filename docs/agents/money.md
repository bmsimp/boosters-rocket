# Money

What this covers: amounts, a family's figures, the money cache, Balances and the money run. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

## Money

- Amounts are integer cents everywhere in the portal. `toCents()` converts a QuickBooks number once, where it comes in, and `formatMoney(cents, currency)` is the only formatter (`src/lib/money/cents.ts`). Never add or compare dollars, and never format an amount yourself. The currency is the invoice's own (`currencyOf`), USD when absent.
- The only invoice the portal ever makes is a volunteer buy-out's (`docs/agents/quickbooks-writes.md`). Once it is made or voided, the family's `family_money` row is marked stale (`stale_at`, checked by `isFresh()`), so the next page load reads it live and it shows among the open invoices like any other.
- A parent page shows money through `loadFamilyMoney(ctx.familyId)` (`src/lib/money/family-money.ts`) and the components in `src/app/(parent)/money-box.tsx`: on Home `InvoiceRows` (each open invoice a To do row with its compact Pay, paused in place under `NotCurrentNote` while the figures are not current) and `HomeTotal` (the total said once, with its source line; with no figures it is `MoneySection`'s words) (Home reads the money before `loadMySeason()`, so a stale row is brought up to date before the payment lines are worked out from it), `MoneySection` and `PaidSection` on Payments, which names a buy-out's invoice ("Paying instead of volunteering: ...") from the family's own buy-outs only (`loadMyBuyoutInvoices(ctx.familyId)`, `src/lib/money/my-buyout-invoices.ts`, only while the family's record is active; an invoice is named only when its id and customer both match). Do not query invoices from a page. A page shows a family's money only when `loadMyFamily()` returns the family: Home and Payments ask it first, and show `NotSetUpBox` and no money when it returns null. `loadFamilyMoney()` takes the app's family and is the one place parent code finds the family's customer for money: the customer its own family link names (`customerOfFamily()`, in the roster's company, and nothing while QuickBooks is connected to another), on each load (it loads before and after a live read, and finds it again the second time, inside the live read's transaction too, and once more after it has taken the family's money lock: since stage 4's slice M3b a link that changed between the first look and the lock, by an unlink or a relink, reads and keeps nothing; the run's `readFamilyForRun()` checks the same after its lock and passes the family over as `relinked`, counted as not read and stamped with nothing). An unlink deletes the family's row at once, under the family's money lock taken after the roster lock (`forgetFamilyMoney()`): the one lock order, since nothing that holds the money lock takes the roster lock (a live read, the run, `readAndKeep()`, `markBuyoutMoneyStale()` and the buy-out confirm checked). The unlink locks the family record `for no key update`, never `for update`, so the money row a read is writing under its lock (whose foreign key takes a key share on the record) never waits on the unlink that waits on the read. With no active record it shows no figures and reads and writes nothing; with no link, with no membership shape, or while the shape names a customer type QuickBooks no longer has (`shapeHolds()` in `src/lib/roster/shape.ts`, shared with a buy-out's billing, the rule `loadMyFamily()` kept for money before the family became the app's record), it shows no figures and no name, asks QuickBooks nothing and writes nothing (`MoneyBox`, which asks `loadMyFamily()` nothing, is safe by this alone). Its answer tells three things apart: figures, fresh or marked not current; `notConnected`, for an active family the portal can tell has no link (`familyLinkState()` in `src/lib/families/links.ts` says `none`: the roster names a company, QuickBooks is not connected to a different one, and the family has no family link in that company; or the roster names no company at all and the family holds no link of any company), decided before the shape is asked about; and every other case with no figures (QuickBooks connected to another company, `unknown`; a roster with no company beside a family that still holds a link, `unknown` too; a shape that doesn't hold; a linked family the cache no longer holds as an active family; a failed read with nothing stored), which keeps its words. `customerOfFamily()` answers nothing in both of the last two: what tells them apart is the roster's company and the connection, read beside the link. For `notConnected` nothing is read from QuickBooks, nothing written and no failed try stamped, and `MoneySection` says "The club hasn't connected your payments to the portal yet. Until then, the treasurer can tell you what you owe." with no amount, Pay, paid list, not-current note or way to Payments; a linked family never reads it, since it would hide what the family owes (`tests/integration/family-money.test.ts`, `payments-page.test.ts`, `parent-home-family.test.ts`). Every payment line of its season reads "No invoice yet" (staff can only waive it today). Staff Balances lists the roster's family customers, so it has no row for the family itself (a customer left with no family record, the old customer of an unlinked family among them, is listed reading "No family in the portal", with no figures and no link to a page), and the family's Balances page answers Not found; since stage 4's slice M3b (the owner's choice 9, which the owner confirmed on 2026-11-21) `loadBalances()` also counts the active family records with no family link in the records' company (`unlinked`, read in `balances.ts` itself, none while that company is unknown), and the page says in one `Notice` that so many families in the portal aren't linked, so have no figures there, with a link to Accounting links for a reader holding `Accounting.Link.ReadWrite` and `Roster.Read`, and otherwise who links them.
- `family_money` holds one row per app family (`family_id`, the key, a `restrict` foreign key; migrations `0032_family_money_app_ids_additive` and `0033_family_money_app_ids_finish`, `tests/integration/migrate-money.test.ts`), written only for a family with a record: the upsert, the stale mark and every failure stamp go by it, and a page load for a family with no record writes nothing. The per-family advisory lock is keyed by the same id in page loads and the run. Inside a row everything stays QuickBooks': each invoice and each customer check names its `customerQboId`, and `realm_id` the company. It holds the last successful read (invoices as shown, total, `BalanceWithJobs`, currency, company, time) and the last failed try. A page load shows a fresh row at once (`money.settings` `{ freshSeconds }`, default 300); otherwise it makes one live read with a short timeout, and on failure shows the last figures marked not current with Pay switched off. The total is the sum of the open invoices shown, not QuickBooks' balance.
- The note on figures that are not current is the same whatever the cause (an outage, a connection only an Admin can mend, another read under way): it never says QuickBooks is down and never promises when paying will be back.
- Whose invoices: the family customer and its active direct sub-customers of the student or parent type (flat: the member alone), read from the roster cache for the customer the session's family is linked to (`customerOfFamily()`, never another link), and only for an active family. It stays the cache's tree in stage 2, the lead's decision: only the row the figures are kept in is the app family's, never which invoices are its. Nothing in a request selects a customer. An id goes into query text only after `isQboId()` (digits only).
- Each stored invoice names the customer it was raised on (`customerQboId`). A row that holds an invoice of a customer the cache no longer puts in the family (a student moved or made inactive) is treated as absent, however fresh (`mayShow`), and saving a membership shape deletes every row.
- `BalanceWithJobs` counts every customer under the family at every depth, so it is read and compared (the credit line, the `money.balance_mismatch` audit) only when the cache holds nothing under the family but the customers whose invoices make up the total (`balanceCoversTheSame`).
- Never the wrong company: no live read unless QuickBooks is connected to the company the roster cache came from, and a stored row from another company is treated as absent.
- A Pay button's address goes through `safePayLink()` (`src/lib/money/pay-link.ts`): https on `intuit.com` or a subdomain, or no button. Due dates are said in words by `DueLine` (`src/app/(parent)/due-line.tsx`) against the club's own date, which `loadFamilyMoney()` returns with the figures (`today`; see The club's date in `docs/agents/operations.md`).
- QuickBooks reads for money go through the client's readers (`qboInvoices`, `qboCustomerById` in `src/lib/quickbooks/client.ts`), which only GET. The invoice query strings (`INVOICE_QUERIES`) have not been tried against a real company yet (roadmap, checks table): do not change them without trying the new string in the sandbox.
- Every query answer is read strictly (`strictRowsOf`), because an empty one is acted on: no invoices reads as "nothing owed", and no customers would empty the roster. Only a 200 whose `QueryResponse` is an object holding no rows means no rows. Any other 200 is refused (`bad_answer`, or `fault` for a Fault), never taken for an empty list. A new read does the same.
- A read made for a page load passes `failFast`. It never waits for the connection row lock: a token that is still good is read without it, and one that needs refreshing is refreshed once for all the page loads in the process, taking the row only if it is free (`busy` otherwise). Never call `accessToken()` from code a parent page runs: it waits its turn for that lock, through another caller's request to Intuit.

- Staff see money on Balances (`/staff/balances`, `Finance.Balance.Read`; each page audited before it shows anything). The worker's read of every family (`runMoneyJob()`, `src/lib/money/sweep.ts`; daily, or when staff ask) goes one family at a time, at most one request every 400 ms, into the same `family_money` rows by the same rules. It may wait for the connection row (parent page loads never do), keeps its place in the setting `money.run` and carries on at the next wake, and writes one audit row per run. A family it stopped at three runs in a row (`STOPS_AT_ONE_FAMILY`, `money.run.stoppedAt`) is passed over by the next run, counted as not read; a later good read clears the count. Staff asking only record the ask (`askForMoneyRun()`); the worker starts it. The run also compares each customer's own `Customer.Balance` with its open invoices (`customer_checks`, "Worth a look" on Balances). `balances.ts`, `sweep.ts` and `items.ts` load every family: staff only (`STAFF_ONLY_IMPORTS`). They sit in `src/lib/money`, which is scanned whole as parent-facing (`PARENT_FACING_LIB`), so they import no staff-only module either (not `everyone.ts`, `subjects.ts` or `roster-import.ts`), and find families through `src/lib/families/links.ts`: the run and `loadBalances()` each turn every roster family customer into its app family with one `linksOfCustomers()`, reading the family map only (a customer may have a student record too; each Balances row and "Worth a look" row carries it, for its link), and `loadFamilyBalance()` takes the app family its page's address names and finds its one customer with `customerOfFamily()`; `items.ts` reads no family. The run walks family customers in customer order and keeps its place (`money.run`: `after`, `startedAfter`, `stoppedAt.qboId`) as customer ids, a money record; a roster family with no family record is passed over (`no_record` in `PASS_OVER`), asking QuickBooks nothing and stamping nothing, counted as not read and in the run's audit row (`passedOverWithNoRecord`), and never stops a run, until staff make a family from it ("Create a family from this", `docs/agents/app-records.md`), when the next run reads it. The Balances family page (`/staff/balances/[familyId]`, by the app's family since stage 3's third slice) answers 404 for anything but a family record whose customer, through its link in the roster's company, is an active family of the roster cache, and audits `money.family.read` naming the app's family (`family`; older rows name the customer as `qbo_customer`, and the audit page's record filter finds both); `money.balance_mismatch` names the app's family too. The daily clean-up deletes a row only when the customer its family record's link names in the roster's company is gone from the cache altogether, never because the family is inactive, and nothing while the cache is empty. The products and services a payment requirement picks from are `qbo_item`, read with `ITEM_QUERY` (not yet tried against a real company) by one reader, `readItems()` (`items.ts`), when staff press and after each daily run that read every family (the worker's money job, `runMoneyAndItems()` in `src/lib/jobs/money-job.ts`: `src/lib/money` is parent-facing, so it may not import `items.ts`). An answer with no items over a stored list that holds some is refused (`no_items`) and the list kept. The list is emptied by `clearMoneyRecords()` on a change of company. A family the run passes over as not a family gets no failed-try stamp; a read that failed does. A stored invoice carries `itemQboIds`; a row stored without them is read again, never guessed at.
