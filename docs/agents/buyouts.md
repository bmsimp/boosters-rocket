# Buy-outs

What this covers: volunteer buy-outs, a bullet of Writes to QuickBooks (`docs/agents/quickbooks-writes.md`), which applies to them too. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

## Buy-outs

- Buy-outs (`src/lib/buyouts/`, the `buyout` table; v1 spec §11.5). Staff offer one on a volunteer requirement's page (`Season.Steps.ReadWrite`): a price per shift or hour still owed (`requirement.buyout_unit_cents`) and the item (`requirement.item_qbo_id`); switching it off leaves chosen ones as they are. A guardian confirms on `/volunteer/pay-instead/[requirementId]/[choice]`. Nothing the browser sends names a customer, an item or an amount: the form carries the requirement id, a choice key the server made from the family's own subjects (`family`, or `s1`, `s2`... for its active students in the order `sessionFamily()` lists them, by name and then the app's id, never by a QuickBooks id) and an HMAC stamp of the subject it showed (`subjectStamp()`: the app's ids of the family, the requirement and the subject, no price) and of the quote it showed, and the action checks the subject first and works the quote out again for the session's family, refusing with the new amount if it differs (rate-limited, `buyout_parent`). The quote's stamp (`quoteStamp()`) seals the app's ids of the family and of the subject beside the requirement, the customer billed, the item and the price, so a quote stamp made the old way (sealing QuickBooks ids) never matches and is answered with the price-changed words, never with an invoice for the wrong student; a page drawn before stage 3's fourth slice bound no subject's stamp (its quote stamp arrives where the subject's should, and no quote stamp at all), so it is answered with the reload words. A key that has come to name another of the family's students since the page was drawn (a student joined, left or was renamed and the order moved) fails the subject's stamp: `confirmMyBuyout()` and `switchMyBuyoutBack()` (whose form carries the requirement id, the key and the subject's stamp) both refuse with the reload words, setting up, reading and voiding nothing, so a stale page never bills or voids for a sibling, and the price-changed words are said only when the price changed. What a parent's buy-out bills is read in one place, `loadTargets()` in `my-buyouts.ts`: the family's own linked customer (`customerOfFamily()`) for a per-family requirement, or the student's own (`customerOfStudent()`) while the roster cache holds it active and directly under the family's (billing follows QuickBooks' tree until QuickBooks takes a move: open question 14); with no link there is no offer: no pay-instead link or price on Volunteer, Not found at every pay-instead address, and the confirm refused as not from the page (`tests/integration/buyouts.test.ts`). Nor is there one while the membership shape doesn't hold (`shapeHolds()` in `src/lib/roster/shape.ts`: no shape, or one naming a customer type QuickBooks no longer has; money's rule too), when the pay-instead page says the family isn't set up and `confirmMyBuyout()` and `switchMyBuyoutBack()` refuse with `BUYOUT_COPY.notSetUp`, the confirm again under the roster lock and the switch-back in its void's transaction, as they did when `loadMyFamily()` went by the shape; a live buy-out still reads in the season's words. A family that has a live buy-out and then loses its link (possible only once stage 4 can unlink a family) still reads the buy-out's words on its requirement line ("Buy-out invoiced" and the rest, from `buyoutsByRecord()`, which goes by the app's records), with nowhere to see it, pay it or switch back: stage 4 decides what it reads, in the slice that unlinks a family (M3b); a test pins today's reading. Since stage 2 of the app-owned families spec a buy-out names the app's own records, never a QuickBooks id for them: `buyout.family_id` (never null) is the family that chose it, and `student_id` the student a per-student requirement's buy-out is for (null for a per-family one, whose subject is the family), each a `restrict` foreign key; what it bills stays QuickBooks' (`customer_qbo_id`, `item_qbo_id`, `invoice_qbo_id`, `invoice_sync_token`, `realm_id`). Migration `0030_buyouts_app_ids_additive` filled them through `accounting_link` by the kind each column holds, in the roster's company, and stops on any row with no record of that kind, of another company, or per-family with a subject that isn't its family; `0031_buyouts_app_ids_finish` drops `family_qbo_id` and `subject_qbo_id` (`tests/integration/migrate-buyouts.test.ts`). In the code they are `AppFamilyId`, `AppStudentId` and `SubjectId` (`subjectOfBuyout()`, `buyoutRecords()` in `src/lib/buyouts/standing.ts`); new audit rows name `familyId` and `studentId` in their body, a move's flag `toFamilyId` too, and `volunteer.buyout.family.read` targets the app's family (older rows keep the QuickBooks ids, and the audit page's record filter finds both: `buyoutRowsOf()` in `src/lib/audit/query.ts`, an old choice's subject customer only as the kind its requirement asks of). At most one live buy-out per requirement and family for a per-family one (`buyout_family_live_uq`) and per requirement and student for a per-student one, whichever family chose it (`buyout_student_live_uq`): partial unique indexes, so one choice makes one invoice; the confirm's insert names neither (`onConflictDoNothing()` untargeted, and a unique violation is known by its code), and re-checks under the roster lock that the family's record is the session's and active, the student's active and in it, and the customer billed still the subject's own linked customer, active and the family's or directly under it. The confirm records the buy-out, the queued create and the audit row (`volunteer.buyout.chosen`) in one transaction, then tries once without waiting for the connection row; the queue (the ops `invoice_create` and `invoice_void`, `QueuedWrite` in `schema.ts`) does the rest. A create is marked `sent` before it goes, so a try cut off mid-send is never simply sent again, and "Try again now" waits out the guardian's own first try. Before it sends again a create that QuickBooks may have made, it reads that customer's invoices strictly and looks for the buy-out's id in the private note: found, it is made. Every void reads the invoice live first and voids only one with nothing paid (balance equal to total); a paid or part-paid one is never voided by the portal. What QuickBooks refuses is not queued: the buy-out is refused and listed on Reconciliation. After each money run the daily check (`checkBuyoutInvoices()`) marks paid ones paid, ends one voided in QuickBooks (`voided_in_quickbooks`), flags an invoice QuickBooks no longer shows and one on another customer, each audited once (a flag a later run doesn't bear out is taken off), and skips figures marked stale since they were read; since slice 9c it voids nothing for a student QuickBooks moved, and flags such a per-student buy-out once (`student_moved`, `flagMovedAway()`), so Reconciliation lists it and its close (`moved_away` in `closeVerdict()`) reads the invoice live first, as `looks_gone` does; that flag is the check's own (`movedAwayByTheCheck()`, `closing.ts`: no void tried beside it), so a later run that finds the student back under the family takes it off, and so does one that finds it paid, and the Health page's buy-out line counts it. Whoever's figures may show a buy-out's invoice (the family that chose it, and the family its student is under now) are marked stale when it is made or voided (`markBuyoutMoneyStale()`): the one that chose it by its own record (`buyout.family_id`), and the family its student is under now through the roster cache's parent and that customer's family link (whose invoices a family sees follows QuickBooks' tree, a move made there included, though no record follows it). Each buy-out's queue item is filed under the buy-out's own family record (`write_queue.family_id`; the create's is the family the confirm checked under the lock, the session's own active record, never anything from the form; a void's is `buyout.family_id`, never null), so the void of an invoiced buy-out is always queued (`queueVoid()` returns no item only for one no longer invoiced); its request names only what is sent (an item queued before migration 0034 also carries the family's customer as `familyQboId`, which nothing reads). A waived requirement, and a student another family's live buy-out still holds, offers no choice; a flagged buy-out offers nothing and says the club is looking; a guardian is told a payment was made only when the live invoice shows one. A move staff make runs `buyoutsForMovedStudent()` from `afterStudentMoved()`, which takes the move by the app's own ids (slice 9f): an unpaid per-student invoice is voided through the queue, a paid one flagged; a per-family one is untouched. The daily check judges no per-student buy-out whose customer the roster cache no longer holds active and directly under the customer its family record is linked to (the family's figures hold none of that customer's invoices): it flags it, as above, and leaves a buy-out whose family has no link for a later run. The requirement reads "Buy-out being set up", "Buy-out invoiced", "Paid instead" (met), "Buy-out being cancelled" or "Buy-out waiting for the club" (`BUYOUT_WORDS`, `src/lib/buyouts/rules.ts`, read by the volunteer rule in `kinds.ts`); confirmed credit still counts as before, except toward a requirement whose buy-out is paid: like a waived one it takes no credit (`allocateFamilies()`), which flows on. Staff close a buy-out from Reconciliation or a family's buy-outs page (`/staff/roster/[id]/buyouts`, by the app's family, `Roster.Read`, audited `volunteer.buyout.family.read` naming it; `loadFamilyBuyouts()` takes the `AppFamilyId` and names the family from the customer its link names, Not found only when it has neither a name nor a buy-out; amounts only with `Finance.Balance.Read`) with `Roster.Family.ReadWrite` (`src/lib/buyouts/close.ts`, `volunteer.buyout.closed`, a reason kept for staff): only where `closeVerdict()` (`closing.ts`, pure) says no unpaid invoice can stand (refused; a stuck create every try of which never sent; an invoice a void's live read found gone; one the daily check flagged `missing`, which the close first reads again live and closes only when it is still gone or voided (`looks_gone`, `invoiceStandsLive()`, a GET); one paid in full that was to be voided, the only close a paid one has), never while a queue item waits or it is being invoiced or voided. A paid one the daily check finds loses the check's own flags (`missing`, `other_customer`). It sends nothing to QuickBooks, ends the buy-out `voided` with `end_reason` `closed_by_staff` (so the family can choose again), gives up its queue items in the same transaction (`giveUpIn()`, `queue.ts`), under the shared roster lock, refusing a buy-out or roster that changed since the page (`closeTag`, `currentRosterTag()`). Every other row says what to do in QuickBooks first.
