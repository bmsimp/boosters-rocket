# Buy-outs

What this covers: volunteer buy-outs, a bullet of Writes to QuickBooks (`docs/agents/quickbooks-writes.md`), which applies to them too. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

## Buy-outs

- Buy-outs (`src/lib/buyouts/`, the `buyout` table; v1 spec §11.5). Staff offer one on a volunteer requirement's page (`Season.Steps.ReadWrite`): a price per shift or hour still owed (`requirement.buyout_unit_cents`) and the item (`requirement.item_qbo_id`); switching it off leaves chosen ones as they are. A guardian confirms on `/volunteer/pay-instead/[requirementId]/[choice]`. Nothing the browser sends names a customer, an item or an amount: the form carries the requirement id, a choice key the server made from the family's own subjects (`family`, or `s1`, `s2`... for its active students in the order `sessionFamily()` lists them, by name and then the app's id, never by a QuickBooks id) and an HMAC stamp of the subject it showed (`subjectStamp()`: the app's ids of the family, the requirement and the subject, no price) and of the quote it showed, and the action checks the subject first and works the quote out again for the session's family, refusing with the new amount if it differs (rate-limited, `buyout_parent`). The quote's stamp (`quoteStamp()`) seals the app's ids of the family and of the subject beside the requirement, the customer billed, the item and the price, so a quote stamp made the old way (sealing QuickBooks ids) never matches and is answered with the price-changed words, never with an invoice for the wrong student; a page drawn before stage 3's fourth slice bound no subject's stamp (its quote stamp arrives where the subject's should, and no quote stamp at all), so it is answered with the reload words. A key that has come to name another of the family's students since the page was drawn (a student joined, left or was renamed and the order moved) fails the subject's stamp: `confirmMyBuyout()` and `switchMyBuyoutBack()` (whose form carries the requirement id, the key and the subject's stamp) both refuse with the reload words, setting up, reading and voiding nothing, so a stale page never bills or voids for a sibling, and the price-changed words are said only when the price changed. What a parent's buy-out bills is read in one place, `loadTargets()` in `my-buyouts.ts`: the family's own linked customer (`customerOfFamily()`) for a per-family requirement, or the student's own (`customerOfStudent()`) while the roster cache holds it active and directly under the family's (billing follows QuickBooks' tree until QuickBooks takes a move: open question 14); with no link there is no offer: no pay-instead link or price on Volunteer, Not found at every pay-instead address, and the confirm refused as not from the page (`tests/integration/buyouts.test.ts`). Nor is there one while the membership shape doesn't hold (`shapeHolds()` in `src/lib/roster/shape.ts`: no shape, or one naming a customer type QuickBooks no longer has; money's rule too), when the pay-instead page says the family isn't set up and `confirmMyBuyout()` and `switchMyBuyoutBack()` refuse with `BUYOUT_COPY.notSetUp`, the confirm again under the roster lock and the switch-back in its void's transaction, as they did when `loadMyFamily()` went by the shape; a live buy-out still reads in the season's words. An unlink (stage 4's slice M3b, `unlinkFamily()` in `src/lib/accounting/link-writes.ts`) is refused while a buy-out chosen by the family or for one of its student records is `being_invoiced`, `invoiced` (whatever the stored figures say: the daily check marks a paid one paid), `being_voided` or `stuck`, in words saying how many, which requirements, that its invoice is on the family's customer, and when the family can be unlinked (the owner's choice 3, which the owner confirmed on 2026-11-21), so no family is left with no link and an unpaid invoice nobody can reach, and the daily check, `closeVerdict()` and the pay-instead pages need no change. A `paid` one never refuses it and is kept: it goes on meeting its requirement and reading "Paid instead" by its own state, with no pay-instead link or page (`tests/integration/buyouts.test.ts`). Since stage 2 of the app-owned families spec a buy-out names the app's own records, never a QuickBooks id for them: `buyout.family_id` (never null) is the family that chose it, and `student_id` the student a per-student requirement's buy-out is for (null for a per-family one, whose subject is the family), each a `restrict` foreign key; what it bills stays QuickBooks' (`customer_qbo_id`, `item_qbo_id`, `invoice_qbo_id`, `invoice_sync_token`, `realm_id`). Migration `0030_buyouts_app_ids_additive` filled them through `accounting_link` by the kind each column holds, in the roster's company, and stops on any row with no record of that kind, of another company, or per-family with a subject that isn't its family; `0031_buyouts_app_ids_finish` drops `family_qbo_id` and `subject_qbo_id` (`tests/integration/migrate-buyouts.test.ts`). In the code they are `AppFamilyId`, `AppStudentId` and `SubjectId` (`subjectOfBuyout()`, `buyoutRecords()` in `src/lib/buyouts/standing.ts`); new audit rows name `familyId` and `studentId` in their body, a move's flag `toFamilyId` too, and `volunteer.buyout.family.read` targets the app's family (older rows keep the QuickBooks ids, and the audit page's record filter finds both: `buyoutRowsOf()` in `src/lib/audit/query.ts`, an old choice's subject customer only as the kind its requirement asks of). At most one live buy-out per requirement and family for a per-family one (`buyout_family_live_uq`) and per requirement and student for a per-student one, whichever family chose it (`buyout_student_live_uq`): partial unique indexes, so one choice makes one invoice; the confirm's insert names neither (`onConflictDoNothing()` untargeted, and a unique violation is known by its code), and re-checks under the roster lock that the family's record is the session's and active, the student's active and in it, and the customer billed still the subject's own linked customer, active and the family's or directly under it. The confirm records the buy-out, the queued create and the audit row (`volunteer.buyout.chosen`) in one transaction, then tries once without waiting for the connection row; the queue (the ops `invoice_create` and `invoice_void`, `QueuedWrite` in `schema.ts`) does the rest. A create is marked `sent` before it goes, so a try cut off mid-send is never simply sent again, and "Try again now" waits out the guardian's own first try. Before it sends again a create that QuickBooks may have made, it reads that customer's invoices strictly and looks for the buy-out's id in the private note: found, it is made. Every void reads the invoice live first and voids only one with nothing paid (balance equal to total); a paid or part-paid one is never voided by the portal. What QuickBooks refuses is not queued: the buy-out is refused and listed on Reconciliation. After each money run the daily check (`checkBuyoutInvoices()`) marks paid ones paid, ends one voided in QuickBooks (`voided_in_quickbooks`), flags an invoice QuickBooks no longer shows and one on another customer, each audited once (a flag a later run doesn't bear out is taken off), and skips figures marked stale since they were read; since slice 9c it voids nothing for a student QuickBooks moved, and flags such a per-student buy-out once (`student_moved`, `flagMovedAway()`), so Reconciliation lists it and its close (`moved_away` in `closeVerdict()`) reads the invoice live first, as `looks_gone` does; that flag is the check's own (`movedAwayByTheCheck()`, `closing.ts`: no void tried beside it), so a later run that finds the student back under the family takes it off, and so does one that finds it paid, and the Health page's buy-out line counts it. Whoever's figures may show a buy-out's invoice (the family that chose it, and the family its student is under now) are marked stale when it is made or voided (`markBuyoutMoneyStale()`): the one that chose it by its own record (`buyout.family_id`), and the family its student is under now through the roster cache's parent and that customer's family link (whose invoices a family sees follows QuickBooks' tree, a move made there included, though no record follows it). Each buy-out's queue item is filed under the buy-out's own family record (`write_queue.family_id`; the create's is the family the confirm checked under the lock, the session's own active record, never anything from the form; a void's is `buyout.family_id`, never null), so the void of an invoiced buy-out is always queued (`queueVoid()` returns no item only for one no longer invoiced); its request names only what is sent (an item queued before migration 0034 also carries the family's customer as `familyQboId`, which nothing reads). A waived requirement, and a student another family's live buy-out still holds, offers no choice; a flagged buy-out offers nothing and says the club is looking; a guardian is told a payment was made only when the live invoice shows one. A move staff make runs `buyoutsForMovedStudent()` from `afterStudentMoved()`, which takes the move by the app's own ids (slice 9f): an unpaid per-student invoice is voided through the queue, a paid one flagged; a per-family one is untouched. The daily check judges no per-student buy-out whose customer the roster cache no longer holds active and directly under the customer its family record is linked to (the family's figures hold none of that customer's invoices): it flags it, as above, and leaves a buy-out whose family has no link for a later run. The requirement reads "Buy-out being set up", "Buy-out invoiced", "Paid instead" (met), "Buy-out being cancelled" or "Buy-out waiting for the club" (`BUYOUT_WORDS`, `src/lib/buyouts/rules.ts`, read by the volunteer rule in `kinds.ts`); confirmed credit still counts as before, except toward a requirement whose buy-out is paid: like a waived one it takes no credit (`allocateFamilies()`), which flows on. Staff close a buy-out from Reconciliation or a family's buy-outs page (`/staff/roster/[id]/buyouts`, by the app's family, `Roster.Read`, audited `volunteer.buyout.family.read` naming it; `loadFamilyBuyouts()` takes the `AppFamilyId` and names the family from the customer its link names, Not found only when it has neither a name nor a buy-out; amounts only with `Finance.Balance.Read`) with `Roster.Family.ReadWrite` (`src/lib/buyouts/close.ts`, `volunteer.buyout.closed`, a reason kept for staff): only where `closeVerdict()` (`closing.ts`, pure) says no unpaid invoice can stand (refused; a stuck create every try of which never sent; an invoice a void's live read found gone; one the daily check flagged `missing`, which the close first reads again live and closes only when it is still gone or voided (`looks_gone`, `invoiceStandsLive()`, a GET); one paid in full that was to be voided and wasn't, flagged `paid_before_void`, `student_moved` or `void_refused` (`paidWhileToBeVoided()`; a paid `given_up` one is a create QuickBooks made after it was given up, paid instead as chosen), the only close a paid one has), never while a queue item waits or it is being invoiced or voided. A paid one the daily check finds loses the check's own flags (`missing`, `other_customer`). It sends nothing to QuickBooks, ends the buy-out `voided` with `end_reason` `closed_by_staff` (so the family can choose again), gives up its queue items in the same transaction (`giveUpIn()`, `queue.ts`), under the shared roster lock, refusing a buy-out or roster that changed since the page (`closeTag`, `currentRosterTag()`). Every other row says what to do in QuickBooks first. A change of company (stage 4's slice M7a, open question 16; still refused until slice M8) ends every unpaid live buy-out (being invoiced, invoiced, being cancelled or stuck, read from its state at the clear) the same way, in the portal alone: `voided` with `end_reason` `earlier_company`, its customer, item, invoice, company, flag and when it was flagged kept, nothing sent to QuickBooks, by one update that takes each row's lock before the clear gives up the queue (`endBuyoutsOfEarlierCompany()`, `src/lib/buyouts/company-change.ts`, audited `volunteer.buyouts.ended` with the counts by state, the paid ones kept and the ids ended), so a `queueVoid()` racing it either committed first (its buy-out ends, its void is given up) or finds the buy-out no longer invoiced and queues nothing; a buy-out write on its way to QuickBooks at that moment can't undo it (stage 4's slice M7a2): every transaction that finishes a try (`created()`, `voided()`, `tryFailed()`, `createRefused()`, `voidKept()`, `src/lib/buyouts/writes.ts`) takes the roster lock shared first, waiting at most 10 seconds (`lockForFinish()`), then the item FOR UPDATE, then the buy-out, and changes the item only while it is waiting, so one given up meanwhile (by the clear, or by staff) stays given up, with no `roster.write.done`, `retry` or `stuck` row; the buy-out's own updates keep their state guards, so one the clear ended is never invoiced, refused, kept or stuck again, and an invoice QuickBooks made for it is kept on its item (`result_qbo_id`) and recorded on the ended buy-out (`invoice_qbo_id`, `invoice_sync_token`, its state unchanged, audited `volunteer.buyout.invoice_found_after_ending`) for slice M7b's list. One staff gave up mid-try is invoiced all the same when QuickBooks made the invoice, its item left given up. A create is marked as maybe sent only while its item waits, and a void is sent only while it waits; one given up before its send sends nothing. A create's invoice is kept on its item (`result_qbo_id`) before its finish waits for the roster lock, in a statement that touches the item alone, so a finish that can't take the lock in time (a roster read holding it, with a change of company's clear after it that gives the item up and ends the buy-out) still leaves the invoice named; the worker's finish waits again, up to 4 minutes, inside its claim's lease, and the guardian's try made at once is told the write is on its way and left to the worker, which looks for the invoice by the buy-out's id before sending anything again. The one lock order for an item and its buy-out is the roster lock shared, the item, the buy-out, then the family's figures: the finishes, a staff close and "Give up" (`giveUp()`, `queue.ts`, which answers in words to try again when the wait runs out) all take it, and the clear holds the roster lock alone, so none of them runs beside it. Its invoice stays in the old company's books for the treasurer. A paid one keeps counting, as it is, and a voided or refused one is history already; the family may choose again for that requirement, as far as the database goes. Since slice M7b each reads true. A buy-out of an earlier company is one whose `realm_id` is not the roster's (so the paid ones kept are read beside the ended ones; none while the roster names no company), and one pure rule, `earlierCompanyLook()` (`closing.ts`), says from the row, its kept flag and last code, and its queue items (`itemsOf()`, which returns each item's `result_qbo_id` too, and whether QuickBooks' answer closed it, `answered`: done, or given up with no note, as `itemClosed()` leaves one, where staff and the clear leave a note) what it leaves to settle in that company's books: `void_may_have_gone` (its newest void not closed by an answer, sent or not: a void is not marked sent before it goes, so one the clear gave up mid-send looks unsent), `part_paid` (a void's read found something paid and the rest owed: last code `paid`), `last_seen_gone` (a void's read found it gone, `invoice_gone`, or the daily check flagged it `missing`), `invoice_stands` (any other known invoice, the buy-out's or else the newest create's kept `result_qbo_id`, a refused void's among them; marked made for a request given up when a given-up create names it, as M7a2's finish leaves one, told from the item and never from the audit log, which Reconciliation's readers may not read), `may_be_made` (no invoice known but a create sent and never answered: the note `Booster Rocket buy-out <id>` to search that company's invoices for), `refund_owed` (paid, flagged `paid_before_void`, `student_moved` or `void_refused`: `paidWhileToBeVoided()`) or `nothing_to_settle` (refused; paid otherwise; ended any other way; its newest void done; or no invoice and no create left unanswered). Two cases it can't tell, since a finish after the clear records nothing (`src/lib/buyouts/writes.ts`, M7b2's or a fix of its own): a create QuickBooks refused after the clear reads `may_be_made`, and a void QuickBooks answered after it `void_may_have_gone`; both say to look there. Reconciliation's "From the earlier QuickBooks company" (`loadEarlierCompanyBuyouts()` in `staff.ts`, `EarlierCompanyBuyouts` in `src/app/staff/(app)/reconciliation/buyouts.tsx`, drawn only with a row and not audited) lists every one the rule doesn't call `nothing_to_settle`, named by the app's own records (`family.name`, `student.name`, through `recordNameWords()`: the old customers are gone from the cache), the family's roster page linked only while it opens (`familyPagesStand()`: not after a clear until the new company's shape is set), with the earlier company, the invoice or the note, the amount only with `Finance.Balance.Read`, a link to the family's season page for its requirement's season, where a waiver is given, and for a refund owed the close as today (`closeVerdict()`'s `paid_in_full`, with `Roster.Family.ReadWrite`); no other row has a press, and nothing marks a row settled (a column, so a slice of its own if the owner asks). Reconciliation's buy-outs list (`loadBuyoutsToLook()`) reads the roster's company's alone. `stateWords()` reads `earlier_company` in words, and the family's buy-outs page reads such a buy-out's invoice as in the earlier company's books (`InvoiceAsRead` `earlier_company`, its id, no figures; no id for `may_be_made`), its student by the record's own name (never the cache's by the old company's customer id, which may name someone else in the new one), the family by its record's name when it has no link, and what the rule says is left to settle (for a refund owed, beside the close). A buy-out ended from a company the club later connects again has that company's `realm_id`, so it leaves the treasurer's list then: slice M8's, which turns a change of company back on. `BUYOUT_WORDS` is for live states and is unchanged: an ended one reads by credit, and once the family is linked again with the requirement's product and the shape the choice is offered, its pay-instead page saying in one line that the family's own last buy-out for it ended with the change of company (`endedForCompany`, a boolean from the family's own rows, `loadTargets()`). `closeVerdict()`, the close and the daily check are unchanged: the check judges only `invoiced` buy-outs of the roster's company, so it touches none of them, and the Health page's buy-out line counts none (`tests/integration/buyouts-check.test.ts`).
