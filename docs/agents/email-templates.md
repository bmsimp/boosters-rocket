# Email templates

What this covers: the words of the emails the portal sends to families, which staff change on Settings, Emails. The spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-email-templates-design.md` (approved by the owner on 2026-11-28, with his four answers). The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

## What is built

- **The catalog** (`src/lib/email-templates/catalog.ts`, pure, no secret, so the editor's page imports it in the browser): the 20 emails, each an `EmailDef` with its key, group, title, when it is sent, its fields (id, label, sample), its fixed parts (what the portal always writes: links, codes, how long they work, the safety lines, the lists, the lines about attachments; each a function of the send's data), whether it may hold links (never in the Sign-in group), its subject (the club's, with fields in braces, or the portal's own for the two code emails, which carry the code), its default words and its samples. Outgoing mail's test (`testMessage()`) is not a template. Every email also offers Club name, Portal address and Club contact (the treasurer's address, or the address the builder already used for "Questions? Write to").
- **The defaults are the words sent before**, byte for byte, for every variant (`tests/unit/mail-templates.test.ts` compares each builder with the copy kept in `tests/helpers/legacy-mail-messages.ts`, which nothing under `src/` imports). A sentence whose grammar changes per send (Strict or Standard, "You're" or "Maya is", one thing or a few) is in a fixed part, so no one has to write variants.
- **The body** (`src/lib/email-templates/body.ts`, pure): `checkTemplate()` rebuilds a template from what was sent: paragraphs, bulleted and numbered lists, bold, italic, underline, hard breaks, links (`https`, `http`, `mailto`, only where the email allows them), the email's own fields, and each of its fixed parts exactly once; a subject of 1 to 200 characters whose braces name its fields. `renderBody()` draws blocks, every piece of text and every value through `esc()`; a paragraph with a field that has no value for this send is left out ("Questions? Write to {Club contact}." for a club with none). `frame.ts` holds the mail's frame and "Powered by Boosters Rocket", which no template reaches.
- **The builders** in `src/lib/mail/messages.ts` keep their signatures: each calls `renderEmail()` with its template. The club's words reach them through `Brand.templates`, which `currentBrand()` (`src/lib/mail/send.ts`) reads with the portal's address and the treasurer's address (`src/lib/mail/template-read.ts`). Saved words that no longer pass this release's rules (a release changed an email's fields or fixed parts) are set aside and the default is sent (`wordsFor()`, `usable` false); the list, the email's page and the Settings rail say "Needs a look".
- **The table** `email_template_version` (migration `0061_email_templates`): one row per save, never changed, `n` from 1 per email, `subject` null where the portal writes it, `reset` for the default saved again; kept by the clean-up. Not keyed by a QuickBooks id.
- **The permission** `Settings.Emails.ReadWrite` is not Admin-only (the owner's answer 3): a custom role (a secretary's) may hold it; ticking it brings `Settings.Club.Read` (`SEE_WITH`), the permission the pages are seen with.
- **The pages**: `/staff/settings/emails` (Settings item `emails`, group Club; each email with "Club's words", "Default" or "Needs a look") and `/staff/settings/emails/[key]` (a key not in the catalog is `notFound()`): the subject and the body in `RichTextEditor`'s email mode, a live preview (the mail's HTML in an `<iframe sandbox srcdoc>` on a light canvas) and the fields with their samples, Save the email, Send me a test, Earlier versions (each at `?version=N`, with Use these words again) and Use the default words behind a `ConfirmDialog`. Someone without the permission sees the preview and fields alone. Not audited: no family's data.
- **The editor's email mode** (`src/app/components/rich-text-editor/`, `email-nodes.tsx`, `insert-field-menu.tsx`): no headings or pictures, no link tool where links aren't allowed; a field is Tiptap's own Mention extension (`@tiptap/extension-mention` and its peer `@tiptap/suggestion`, pinned at 3.33.0) named `field`, typed with `{` or put in from Insert field (the template's dropdown menu); a fixed part is an atom block that a cut or a drag moves and nothing else removes. The server checks again.
- **Writes** (`src/lib/mail/template-store.ts`): `saveEmailTemplate()` (refuses words the same as the current ones; one save of an email at a time, under an advisory lock; audited `email_template.save` with the version, never the words), `restoreDefaultWords()`, `saveVersionAgain()`, and `sendTemplateTest()`: the words on the page, saved or not, filled with the samples, to the staff member's contact email (else their sign-in address), the subject starting "Test: ", refused while outgoing mail isn't working and after 10 in an hour (counted from the person's own `email_template.test` audit rows, so no rate-limit kind was added). A sample link opens a page that refuses it; a sample code is 123456.
