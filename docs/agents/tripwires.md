What this covers: the tripwire tests. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

Since stage 3's slice 11b the first write (`qboUpdateCustomerContact`) is named only by the client, the check of the portal's requests and `src/lib/roster/writes.ts`, called once in its `sendWrite()` (`MAY_SEND_THE_WRITE`), and the contact edits and `src/lib/parents/contact-queue.ts` are held to naming no write and importing nothing from the client (`contactEditsProblems()`, `CONTACT_EDITS_EXPORTS`, which holds `saveDetails`).
