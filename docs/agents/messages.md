# Club messages

What this covers: messages staff post to every family, their versions, files, read state and mail, the club's email switch and a staff member's contact email. The spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-club-messages-design.md`, built in the slices of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-23-club-messages.md`; until a slice ships, this document describes what is built. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

## What is built (slice 1, foundations)

- The tables (migration `0057_club_messages`, `src/lib/db/schema.ts`): `message` (exported as `clubMessage`: `message` is a common local name, an HMAC's input or an action's answer, that `tests/unit/audit-rows.test.ts` would take for the table), `message_version`, `message_file`, `message_version_file`, `message_read`, `message_mail`, and `staff_user.contact_email`. A message row is never deleted: an Admin's delete erases its versions' content and files and stamps `deleted_at` with `deleted_by` (a check holds them together). A version is never changed; `n` is unique per message, from 1, and a title is 1 to 120 characters. A file is held (`held_by`, and `message_id` for an edit or null for a new message) until a post or edit saves it, or posted (`held_by` null, `message_id` set): a check refuses one that is neither. Its `type` is one of `MESSAGE_FILE_TYPES` (`src/lib/messages/file-types.ts`, a short name such as `pdf`, from which the file route builds its MIME type and its fixed name `attachment.<ext>`), at most `MESSAGE_FILE_MAX_BYTES` (10 MB, the check's number too). `message_read` is one row per message and parent, gone with the person; `message_mail` one row per version and address, the address lower-cased and trimmed (a check), staff only. None is keyed by a QuickBooks id: a change of company clears nothing here.
- The daily clean-up (`src/lib/jobs/cleanup.ts`): a held file over `HELD_MESSAGE_FILE_HOURS` (24) that no version lists, and the mail rows of a version saved over `MESSAGE_MAIL_DAYS` (90) ago. The other four tables are kept.
- Permissions (`src/lib/permissions/registry.ts`, words and the area Messages in `words.ts`): `Messages.Post.ReadWrite` (a custom role may hold it: see every message and its mail results, post, edit one's own), and the Admin-only `Messages.Delete.ReadWrite` and `Settings.Messages.ReadWrite`.
- The club's switch (`src/lib/settings/messages.ts`): the setting `messages.email_allowed`, `{ emailAllowed: boolean }`, off until an Admin turns it on; anything stored but `true` reads as off. `setMessagesEmailAllowed()` audits `settings.messages.change` with before and after, and writes nothing when the value is already set. Its Settings item `messages` (group Club) is seen with `Settings.Club.Read` and changed with `Settings.Messages.ReadWrite`; someone who may see it but not change it gets the line "Only an Admin can change this." Its rail status is "Emailing on" or "Emailing off".
- A staff member's contact email (`src/lib/staff/contact-email.ts`, the card on `/staff/account`, `setMyContactEmailAction`, a `staffSelfAction` that takes no id): null means the sign-in address, and an address typed equal to the sign-in address is stored as null, so a later change of sign-in address is followed. Audited `staff.contact_email.change` with the address shown before and after and whether it was the person's own (`own`).

## What is built (slice 2, staff posting)

- The editor is Tiptap 3.33.0 (`@tiptap/react`, `@tiptap/pm`, `@tiptap/starter-kit`, `@tiptap/extension-image`, and the template's other `@tiptap/*` packages, each pinned with no range), in the shared client component `RichTextEditor` (`src/app/components/rich-text-editor/`, `docs/agents/ui.md`): since the owner's changes of 2026-11-23, a thin copy of Tiptap's MIT Simple Editor template, which is kept unchanged in `src/app/components/tiptap/` (its `README.md`), restyled by our own `rich-text-editor.scss`. The form `src/app/staff/(app)/messages/message-form.tsx` holds it and is given only what it shows (the number of families, and for an edit the message's id and its current version's title, body and files). StarterKit runs without code, quotes, rules and strike-through, headings at levels 2 and 3, links only to what `safeHref()` allows. The toolbar keeps only what `checkBody()` keeps: undo and redo, the heading menu (levels 2 and 3), the list menu (bulleted and numbered), bold, italic, underline, the link popover and the template's image upload button. A picture is the template's standard Image while it is in the editor, its `src` the staff file route's address of an uploaded file (`staffFileHref()`); the upload box sends it through the form's upload (`POST /staff/messages/files` below, with the form's limits and refusals, said through the box's `onError`; a file that turns out not to be a picture is kept as an attachment and said so), and a selected picture shows a field for its description (`alt`, `picture-description.tsx`). On the way out `toBody()` turns each picture into `{ attachment, alt }`, its id read back from its address (`staffFileIdOf()`; any other address gives no id, which `checkBody()` refuses), and drops an upload box still open; `fromBody()` does the reverse for an edit. There is no preview (the owner's change of 2026-11-23): the editor is what staff see. Nothing else loads Tiptap: the pages and (slice 3) the parent pages draw a body with `renderBody()`.
- The body (`src/lib/messages/body.ts`, pure, in `PARENT_CODE_ELSEWHERE`): `checkBody(json, pictures)` rebuilds the document from the allowlist of spec section 6 and refuses anything else (a node or mark off the list, an attribute off the list that carries a value: one the editor writes as null is ignored), a link not `https:`, `http:` or `mailto:` by `URL` or over 2,000 characters (its other attributes dropped), an image that isn't `{ attachment, alt }` with the id of one of `pictures` (the message's own pictures: held by the poster for this form, or in its current version), more than 100 KB of JSON or 20,000 characters of text, lists nested more than 12 deep; control characters but a tab are dropped from text (jsonb can't hold a NUL). A body must say something (the owner's change of 2026-11-23, in place of slice 2's title-only message): `hasContent()` is true for some text that isn't only spaces, or a picture; `postMessage()` and `editMessage()` refuse a body without either, files attached or not, with `NO_BODY` ("Add a few words or a picture to the message."), and the form asks the same before it sends. `checkBody()` itself keeps an empty body, as `storedBody()` draws one. `storedBody()` checks a stored body again on the way out against its version's pictures, drawing nothing of one that no longer passes. `renderBody()` returns React elements, every piece of text a text node, never HTML: headings one level down (`h3`, `h4`) under the message's title, a picture by `fileHref(id)`, a run of text under one link as one `<a rel="noopener noreferrer">` followed by its address when its words differ from it.
- The files (`src/lib/messages/files.ts`, `file-types.ts`): `fileTypeOf()` reads the type from the bytes alone: JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP by their magic numbers, PDF by `%PDF-`, the newer Office formats by a ZIP's central directory naming `[Content_Types].xml` and the part (`word/document.xml`, `xl/workbook.xml`, `ppt/presentation.xml`), the older ones by an OLE compound file whose root storage holds `WordDocument`, `Workbook` (or `Book`) or `PowerPoint Document` (the root's own children, never an embedded object's). `checkUpload()` refuses an empty file, one over 10 MB and one of no allowed type, each in a sentence naming it; the name kept is its last part, without control characters, at most 200 characters. At most `MESSAGE_FILES_MAX` (10) files per version, pictures in the body included. The route builds the MIME type (`MESSAGE_FILE_MIME`) and the fixed name (`fixedFileName()`, `attachment.<ext>`) from the stored type.
- Uploads go through a staff route handler, `POST /staff/messages/files` (`src/app/staff/(app)/messages/files/route.ts`, beside the file route, in `ROUTE_HANDLERS`), never an action: an action's body limit is one setting for every action, the before-sign-in ones included, so `next.config.ts` keeps Next.js's defaults (1 MB for an action, 10 MB through the proxy; `tests/unit/pwa-static.test.ts` pins both as unset). The body is the file's bytes alone (`application/octet-stream`, so the whole of a 10 MB file fits under the proxy's 10 MB), and the query string carries `name` and `message` (for an edit); nothing the browser says about the files its form lists is read. The handler checks, in order: the staff session and `Messages.Post.ReadWrite` (refused in one sentence, 403, the permission's refusal audited `access.refused`), the request's `Origin` against `APP_URL` (as Next.js checks an action's), a `Content-Length` (411 without one) of at most `MESSAGE_FILE_MAX_BYTES` (413 before a byte is read), then the bytes counted as they arrive, dropped as soon as they pass the length they gave; then `uploadFile()` as before. It answers JSON in an action's shape (`{ ok: true, message, file }` or `{ ok: false, errors }`), `no-store`. The form (`message-form.tsx`) sends it with `fetch` and refuses a file over 10 MB before sending. A file is held by its uploader (`held_by`, with `message_id` for an edit) until the post or edit saves it. `uploadFile()` counts the limits from the database, under a lock of the staff member's own: an upload is refused when they already hold `MESSAGE_FILES_MAX` (10) files for that message (for a new message, every file they hold with no message), and when what they hold unposted in all, across every message, would pass `MESSAGE_HELD_MAX_BYTES` (100 MB). A held file counts until it is posted, removed, or cleared by the daily clean-up after a day. Removing a held file (`removeHeldFileAction`) deletes it, audited `message.file.remove`; a posted file is never deleted by the form, an edit simply leaves it out.
- `src/lib/messages/staff.ts` (staff only, `STAFF_ONLY_IMPORTS`): `listMessages()`, `staffMessage()` (every version, newest first; a deleted message's content is not read), `familiesReached()` (active families, for "This goes to N families"), `staffFile()` (a posted file of a message that isn't deleted, or a held one for its holder alone), `uploadFile()`, `removeHeldFile()`, `postMessage()` and `editMessage()`. A post is version 1; an edit is the next version, the poster's alone, checked against the session's staff member whatever their permissions (anyone else, an Admin included, is refused and audited `access.refused` with `reason` `not_poster`, as the edit page refuses them), its files those of the current version and those the poster holds for the message; an edit that changes nothing is refused. A version is never changed. Audited `message.post`, `message.edit` (the version, how many files, `emailed`), `message.file.upload` (type, size, the message) and `message.file.remove`: no audit row holds a title, a body or a file's name, since an Admin's delete (slice 4) erases them. `emailed` is false until slice 4.
- The staff pages, each `requireStaffPage("Messages.Post.ReadWrite")`: `/staff/messages` (the list, newest first, a deleted one by who and when), `/new`, `/[id]` (the current version, its earlier ones a list of links, each to `?version=N`, found among the message's own, Edit for the poster alone) and `/[id]/edit` (the poster alone). Not audited: they show no one family's data. The menu entry Messages is in the Families group. The staff file route `/staff/messages/files/[id]` (`ROUTE_HANDLERS`): the session and the permission first, then the file as `staffFile()` allows, `no-store`, `nosniff`, a picture inline and anything else `attachment.<ext>`; not audited.
- The browser run's seed (`scripts/seed-e2e.ts`) holds one message the seeded Admin posted (`E2E_MESSAGE`, `tests/e2e/messages-seed.ts`), so `tests/e2e/staff-pages.spec.ts` opens its pages.

## What is built (slice 3, parents)

- `src/lib/messages/parent.ts` (parent code, `PARENT_CODE_ELSEWHERE`; it imports nothing staff-only): every read takes the parent's id, which a page passes from the session alone (`ctx.parentId`), never from the address. `myMessages()` (every message that isn't deleted, newest first, its current title, poster, "edited" when it has more than one version, and `isNew` for this parent), `myMessagesSummary()` (Home's counts), `openMyMessage()` (one message with every version, newest first, and each version's files; it marks the message read for this parent at its current version as it is read: one `message_read` row per message and parent, moved on to the version shown and its time on each open), `staffProfile()` and `parentFile()`. A deleted message is in none of them. "New" means this parent has no read row, or (slice 4) has one for a version older than the message's latest edit that was emailed (`emailed` and `n` over 1): an emailed edit makes it new again, a quiet one doesn't.
- Read and new are a person's: two guardians of one family each have their own, and a family that joins later sees every message that isn't deleted. A parent reading a message is not audited.
- The parent pages, each `requireParentPage()` with its own address first: `/messages` (no tab: Home leads to it), `/messages/[id]` (the current version, "Posted by <name> · <date>" with the name linking to the profile, "Edited <date>" and Earlier versions, a list of links each to that version with its own files (`?version=N`, found among the message's own; anything else reads the current one); a deleted message or an id that isn't one is `notFound()`), `/messages/from/[staffId]` (the name, the contact email as a `mailto:` link, else the sign-in address, and their messages; only for a staff member with a message that isn't deleted, anyone else `notFound()`, so the club's staff can't be listed). The pieces are in `src/app/(parent)/messages/message-parts.tsx`.
- Home (`src/app/(parent)/page.tsx`): for a family the portal may show, a card under the heading while this parent has new messages ("2 new messages from the club", the newest new one's title, and the way to Messages), a quiet line "Messages" once all are read, nothing when there are none. Not beside the not-set-up box.
- The parent file route `/messages/files/[id]` (`ROUTE_HANDLERS`): `parentLayoutCtx()` first, and without a parent session (a staff one included) the same 404 as for anything else, never a redirect; then only a file a version of a message that isn't deleted lists, earlier versions' included (never a held one). `no-store`, `nosniff`, a picture inline and anything else `attachment.<ext>`; not audited.
- Tests: `seedTwoFamilies()` seeds one message (`tests/helpers/messages.ts`, posted by a staff member of its own) that B's parent has read and A's hasn't; the read row's id is B's marker, and `tests/integration/parent-isolation.test.ts` has the three pages in `NO_FAMILY_DATA` (a message is the club's) with Home's card or line in `MUST_SHOW`. `tests/integration/messages-parent.test.ts` covers read state per parent, the card, profiles and the file route. The e2e seed's Admin has a fixed id (`E2E_MESSAGE.posterId`) for the profile's params in `tests/e2e/parent-params.ts`.

## What is built (slice 4, email and delete)

- The box: while the club allows email (`messagesEmailAllowed()`), the form shows "Also email families" for a post and "Email families about this change" for an edit, ticked, with "Goes to N addresses." first in its hint, beside it, for a post and an edit alike (`emailReach()`: the count, or null while the club doesn't allow it, which leaves the box out). The action sends `email: true` alone as asking; `postMessage()` and `editMessage()` read the switch again in their own transaction, so nothing is queued while it is off, whatever the form sent. The version's `emailed` says it was queued.
- The queue (`src/lib/messages/mail.ts`, staff only, `STAFF_ONLY_IMPORTS`): `queueMail(tx, versionId)` in the post's or edit's transaction writes one `message_mail` row per address: the sign-in address of every person who isn't blocked and has a guardian row that counts at the gate (`guardianRowCounts()`, the rule the reminders use), each address once however many families it is in. The audit row (`message.post`, `message.edit`) says `emailed` and how many `addresses`, never one.
- An edit and the email still queued for earlier versions (`carryUnsentMail()`, in the edit's own transaction, after its version is written): an emailed edit deletes those rows, so each address gets this edit's email alone, exactly one for the edit; a quiet edit moves them onto the new version (each address once), so addresses that never got anything get the message as it now is, as an ordinary first email: a version's mail is an update (`club_message_update`, "Updated: <title>") only when the version is an emailed edit (`emailed` and `n` over 1). The worker checks again: a row whose version is no longer the message's newest is dropped, unsent. The results (`mailResults()`, `latestMailCounts()`) are per version that has mail rows, emailed or moved onto it.
- The worker (`sendMessageMail()`, at each wake of `runDueJobs()`, job `messages.mail`, after the due-date reminders): while outgoing mail works (`mailWorking()`) and the club's switch is on (`messagesEmailAllowed()`, read again at each wake: while an Admin has it off, every queued row waits, not sent, not dropped, its tries untouched, and goes once it is on again; the Settings item says so), under one advisory lock so two wakes never send at once, at most `MESSAGE_MAIL_BATCH` (20) queued rows that are due (`next_try_at` null or past), oldest first; each is read again (only while still `queued`) right before it is sent, then marked `sent`. Only a refusal of the address (`recipientRefused`) is the address's own: it counts a try, keeps the row `queued` with its reason for the next wake, and at the third (`MESSAGE_MAIL_TRIES`) marks it `failed`; nothing else ever marks a row failed or counts a try. A failure that is the mail's own (the server can't be reached, it sends nothing) counts no try: it ends the wake's batch, keeps its reason on that row, and puts every queued row off (`next_try_at`, migration `0058_message_mail_backoff`, never sooner than a row already waits) by `outageWaitMinutes()`: 5 minutes for a row not put off before, then 15 while it is under 20 minutes old, then 60, so an outage is asked about once in 5, 15, then every 60 minutes and uses up no address's tries. Two refused addresses with nothing sent also end the batch (a server refusing to relay). Mail purposes `club_message` and `club_message_update` (an edit). Each wake that sent or tried something is audited `message.mail.sent` with counts. A send that fails is also audited `mail.failed` by `sendMail()`, as every mail's is. There is no Health line: the results are on the message's staff page. A worker stopped between a claim and its mark sends that one email again at a later wake.
- The mail (`clubMessageMail()`, `src/lib/mail/messages.ts`, in the shared frame with the club's bar and the Powered by line): subject the title, or "Updated: <title>"; "<poster> posted a message" (or "updated"); the title; the body by `bodyMail()` (`src/lib/messages/body.ts`: plain text, and HTML in which every piece of text and attribute goes through the mail's `esc()`, never Tiptap's HTML), each picture a line "There's a picture here: view it in the portal."; the names of the files not shown as pictures, with "Open the message in the portal to download them"; and the message's address, `APP_URL` and `/messages/<id>`, alone on its line. No file is attached, no image, nothing loaded.
- The results (staff only): the message's page has an Email card, per emailed version "Version 2: Emailed 142 · 3 couldn't be delivered" (or "Emailing: 40 sent, 102 waiting"), with a table of the failed addresses and their reasons (`mailResults()`); the list has an Email column with the latest emailed version's words (`latestMailCounts()`, `mailWords()`). Not audited: a list across families, like the roster's.
- Delete (`deleteMessage()` in `staff.ts`, `deleteMessageAction`, `Messages.Delete.ReadWrite`, Admin-only): on the message's page, for whoever holds the permission, a Delete card whose button asks first (`delete-message.tsx`: families stop seeing it, no email is sent, its email not yet sent is dropped, it can't be brought back). One transaction removes every version (their file lists and mail rows go with them, on delete cascade), every file of the message (posted, or held for an edit), every parent's read row, and stamps `deleted_at` and `deleted_by`; the staff list keeps "Deleted by <name> on <date>". Audited `message.delete` with the number of versions and files and of emails not yet sent, never the title or any content. A queued email of a deleted message is never sent: its row is gone, and the worker's claim finds nothing.
- Tests: `tests/integration/messages-mail.test.ts` (the box, the queue, the worker, retries, the results, an emailed edit and new, the delete), `bodyMail()` in `tests/unit/message-body.test.ts`, the mail in `tests/unit/mail-messages.test.ts`. `seedTwoFamilies()` also gives the club's message a failed mail row for B's address, its id and reason among B's markers.
- The browser journey (`tests/e2e/messages.spec.ts`, spec section 13, at 390 and 1440 wide, each page checked with axe for WCAG A and AA but contrast, which `parent-a11y.spec.ts` measures in every mode, and for sideways scrolling): the seeded Admin posts with "Also email families" ticked (the spec turns the club's switch on and puts it back) and its email is queued for Zelda; Zelda sees the Home card, opens the message from Messages (no longer "New" after), and opens the poster's profile; the Admin edits it, emailed, and Zelda sees it new again, "Edited" and Version 1 in Earlier versions; the Admin deletes it, its versions and mail are gone, and Zelda's link is a 404. Each pass posts a message of its own, titled with its pass, and removes it after; the seeded message is never opened. It doesn't upload a picture or a PDF: those are the integration tests'.
