# Seasons

What this covers: seasons, steps, requirements and their kinds. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

## Seasons

- A season has steps, a step has requirements, and progress is one `requirement_progress` row per requirement and family or student (a tick, a waiver with its reason, or both). Since stage 2 of the app-owned families spec every season record that names a family or student names the app's own record, never a QuickBooks id: `requirement_cover` and `requirement_progress` hold exactly one of `family_id` and `student_id` (which one follows the requirement's `applies_to`), and `requirement_reminder` a `family_id`, each a `restrict` foreign key, with checks that a row holds one of the two (`*_one_subject`; migrations `0024_seasons_app_ids_additive`, which filled them through `accounting_link` by the kind each requirement asks for and stops on any row with no record of that kind, and `0025_seasons_app_ids_finish`, which drops the QuickBooks columns; `tests/integration/migrate-seasons.test.ts`). In the code they are `SubjectId` (`AppFamilyId | AppStudentId`, `src/lib/seasons/progress.ts`), and `pairKey()` keys by them. A payment requirement's product (`requirement.item_qbo_id`) stays QuickBooks' (open question 5). The tables hold all four kinds: form, payment, volunteer, checklist.
- How a kind is met is one entry in `KIND_RULES` (`src/lib/seasons/kinds.ts`), the only place that knows. All four are built, so staff can create every kind: a checklist item is met by a staff tick ("Done", "Not done yet"); a form when Documenso says its document is COMPLETED (`formRule`, `src/lib/forms/kind.ts`: "Signed", "Not signed yet", "Waiting for the other guardian", "Signed, being finished", "Declined", and "Not ready yet" while it has no template), read from the family's own requests only; a payment when invoices carrying its QuickBooks item (`requirement.item_qbo_id`) exist for the family or student and none is open (`paymentStanding()`, `src/lib/money/payment-rule.ts`: "Paid", "Not paid yet ($X left to pay)", "No invoice yet"); volunteering when confirmed credit reaches the amount in shifts or hours (`volunteerStanding()`, `src/lib/volunteer/rules.ts`, fed by `allocateCredit()`: "1 of 2 shifts done, signed up for 1", "4 of 6 hours done"; a staff tick no longer counts for volunteering). The words depend on who is looking (`Viewing`, passed to `loadFacts()` and `loadFamilyStanding()`): a form waiting for another guardian reads "Not signed yet" to a guardian who still has to sign it; a payment says its amount on a parent's page only from figures within `freshSeconds`, and on a staff page only with `Finance.Balance.Read` ("Not paid yet" otherwise). What a form points at is the jsonb `requirement.form` (the template's id, title and last-changed time, any or every guardian); a volunteer requirement points at nothing: shifts name it in `volunteer_shift_requirement`. The rules that turn standings into "done" (`src/lib/seasons/progress.ts`) are pure and unit-tested: a step is done when every required item that covers the family or student is met or waived.
- A parent page reads its season only through `loadMySeason(ctx)` (`src/lib/seasons/my-season.ts`): the session's family (`ctx.familyId`) and its active students as the app's records (`sessionFamily()`), with no link and no roster cache; nothing while the family's record is inactive. A requirement that covers nobody in the family is dropped there, title and all. The staff page for one family reads through the same `loadFamilyStanding()` (`recorded.ts`), so the two can't disagree; its address names the app's family (stage 3's third slice; `familyRecordAt()` first), found as the season keeps it in one place (`seasonFamilyAt(familyId)`, `src/lib/seasons/subjects.ts`, beside `loadSeasonFamilies()`, the staff side's one reader of the club's families; Not found unless its record is active). Its read is audited naming the app's family (`season.progress.family.read`, target `family`); a tick's, waiver's and chosen list's rows name `subjectId` and `familyId` (open question 17: older rows keep the QuickBooks ids they were written with).
- The staff side's families are `loadSeasonFamilies()` (`src/lib/seasons/subjects.ts`, staff only; with `seasonFamilyAt()` for one): every active `family` with its active `student`s (by `student.family_id`), by name as the roster lists them (case and accents ignored; names that compare equal by their linked customers' QuickBooks ids as numbers, as the roster and the Families pages break the tie, a record with no link first, then a record's id; links are read only for such a tie), named by `recordNameWords()` where the portal knows no name. It reads the app's own tables alone (stage 3's sixth slice): no link, no membership shape, no QuickBooks connection, with one exception kept on purpose until stage 4: while QuickBooks is connected to a company other than the roster cache's (`connectedToAnotherCompany()`, `src/lib/families/links.ts`, the meaning `recordsCompany()` uses), it lists nobody and `seasonFamilyAt()` finds nobody, as the reader did before through `loadRosterPage()`, so no page acts on another company's customers and no job mails them (nothing the portal runs makes that state since stage 2, but a database may be in it from before, until an Admin disconnects; stage 4's mapping screen resolves it). Otherwise a family with no customer is listed and usable while its record is active (nothing makes a record with no link inactive since stage 3's slice 9c: `season-families.test.ts` pins it), and the season, volunteering and forms pages, the reminders and the staff home's counts stand while the shape is broken or QuickBooks is disconnected (`tests/integration/season-families.test.ts`; the exception there and in `due-reminders.test.ts`). It carries no QuickBooks id: a caller that needs a customer for what is QuickBooks' own asks `src/lib/families/links.ts` (the payment rule and the money reads find theirs themselves). Since slice 9c the records stand alone, so it gives the app's families and students, which a change made in QuickBooks no longer changes.
- Parents read "set" (the ruler's word); staff read "step". States are words with an icon (`StateWords`, `src/app/components/state-words.tsx`): done, to do, waived.
- A step or requirement with any tick or waiver is refused removal until staff clear them. Kind and applies-to are fixed once a requirement is made. A step's requirements are reordered with `moveRequirement()` (`season.requirement.move`), as steps are with `moveStep()`. Copying a season copies each requirement's buy-out, form template and reminder schedule.
- A staff write that names families or students (a tick, a waiver, a chosen list) takes the app's own ids from the page, each a uuid (`NOT_FROM_PAGE` otherwise), and acts on one only once it is found among the app's active families and students (`loadSeasonFamilies()`) or the rows already recorded against the requirement. It reads them inside its transaction after `lockRosterAs(tx, tag)` (`src/lib/seasons/roster-lock.ts`): it holds the roster lock, so no refresh (which replaces the roster cache) or change of company runs between the check and the write, and it refuses when the page that sent the ids was shown another company's roster. The page carries `currentRosterTag()`, read before the roster. A chosen list is copied with a season as the same records.
- The boundaries into the areas that have not moved yet (still keyed by QuickBooks ids until their own stage 2 slice, which removes its part). Every kind rule (`KIND_RULES`, each an `AppKindRule`) is asked by the app's own ids, as the season keeps them, and answers by `pairKey()`, so `factsFrom()` (`recorded.ts`), and with it `loadFacts()`, `loadFamilyStanding()` and `everyonesFacts()`, translates nothing; a checklist tick is the season's own record and needs no link. The payment rule reads the figures by the app's family, but whose invoices are a family's, and which are a student's own, is still the roster cache's tree of customers, so `paymentStandings()` (`src/lib/money/payment-standings.ts`) turns the records it is asked about into customers itself, in one place (`figuresBySubject()`), and reads each family's row by the record its family customer's own link names: through `linksOf()` for the records asked about, or, for staff asking about everyone, one `linksOfCustomers()` over the customers of every family the cache holds (`familiesCustomers(null)`), never a query per family and never a staff-only import (`src/lib/money` is parent-facing). A family record is the customer its own link names, and a student record the customer its own link names among that family's customers other than the family's own, whose invoices alone are the student's (`invoicesFor()`); whether a line is the family's or a student's follows the requirement's `appliesTo`. A record with no link in the records' company (none while QuickBooks is connected to another) has no figures there and reads "No invoice yet", never another family's. The forms matrix and form reminders (`src/lib/signed-forms/`) read the season and the requests by the app's ids alike (`everyCover()`, `everyRecorded()` in `everyone.ts`) and the club's families as `loadSeasonFamilies()` reads them from the app's own records: no translation remains there. Buy-outs name the app's records too: `loadMyBuyouts()` (`src/lib/buyouts/my-buyouts.ts`) finds the session's family and students once (`sessionFamily()`, the volunteer pages' one definition of the session's family) and reads the chosen lists, standings, credit and buy-outs by them, and volunteering reads whether a buy-out stands through `buyoutsByRecord()` (`src/lib/volunteer/bought.ts`, used by the volunteer rule and `allocateFamilies()`), which asks `buyoutFacts()` by the same records; what remains of buy-outs at the edge is QuickBooks': the customer billed, the family's and its students' linked customers under it in the roster cache (`docs/agents/buyouts.md`). The stored figures are read by the buy-out's own family record (`buyoutFacts()`, the daily check, the staff pages; `markBuyoutMoneyStale()` marks the chooser's by it). `MySeason` names each student by the app's id alone: no student's QuickBooks id is in it, nor in a React key or map key of Home, Season, the ruler or Volunteer. `loadMyFamily()` reads the family's name and its active students from the app's records (`family.name`, `student.name`, `recordNameWords()` where the portal knows none; `MyStudent` is `{ id: AppStudentId, name }`), and `me` from the portal's own person or family row (stage 3's slice 11a; `docs/agents/quickbooks-writes.md`), none of it from the roster cache; the Home students box matches a standing by the student's id, never by name (the forms pages find a request by the app's ids too); nothing on Home or Family says the details came from QuickBooks (`RosterAsOf` went in slice 11a: the family's name, its students and the contact details are all the app's own records, which a read of QuickBooks doesn't change); the due-date reminders read the money cache by the app's family, for the family and each of its students. The audit target type `family` holds a customer's id in old rows only (those the season's and volunteering's pages wrote before they moved, and `money.balance_mismatch` rows written before the money cache moved); every new row under it names the app's family, and `CUSTOMER_TARGET_TYPES` keeps `family` for the old ones. The follow-ups when staff move a student (`afterStudentMoved()`; since slice 9c a refresh follows none) touch no season record: a tick or chosen list names the student's own record, which moves with them. They take the move by the app's own ids since stage 3's slice 9f (the student, the family left and the family joined, as `moveStudent()` saves it), reading no link, so they run the same for a student or family with no link: `volunteerForMovedStudent()`, `formsForMovedStudent()` (`src/lib/forms/moved.ts`) for requests, and `formsForGoneSigner()` takes the app's family itself, the closed guardian row's own `family_id` (a row with none, one a change of company closed, has no forms to start again, as its link answered nothing before); `buyoutsForMovedStudent()` the same for buy-outs. What stage 2 left keyed by QuickBooks ids, for stage 3 with the session and the roster: the roster cache and whose invoices a family sees. The write queue's family moved in stage 3's second slice (`docs/agents/quickbooks-writes.md`), the addresses of the staff pages about one family in its third (`AGENTS.md`, Checklists, New staff page), and the session's stored family and a pending email change's family in its fifth (`docs/agents/app-records.md`; the identity carries the app's family since its fourth).
