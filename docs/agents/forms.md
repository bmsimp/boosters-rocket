# Forms (Documenso)

What this covers: forms signed in Documenso and their evidence. The rules of `AGENTS.md` still apply.

## Forms (Documenso)

- Forms are signed in the club's own Documenso (v1 spec §9 as corrected by `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-documenso-verification.md`). `src/lib/documenso/client.ts` is its only client: server only, a timeout on every call, errors as short codes, nothing logged, no token in any message. Its address, API token and webhook secret are Forms settings (`/staff/settings/forms`, `Settings.Forms.ReadWrite`, Admin only), the secrets sealed; the address must be exactly the origin Documenso is configured with.
- Since stage 2 of the app-owned families spec a request names the app's own records, never a QuickBooks id: `signature_request.family_id` (never null) is the family whose request it is, and `student_id` the student a per-student form is signed for (null for a per-family form, whose subject is the family), each a `restrict` foreign key; one request per form and subject (`signature_request_family_uq`, `signature_request_student_uq`). The current company's evidence names them too (`signed_form_evidence.family_id`, `student_id`) beside its own copies of the QuickBooks ids (`family_qbo_id`, `subject_qbo_id`, `subject_kind`, `realm_id`), which are never changed, removed or used to find a family; an earlier company's names neither (`signed_form_evidence_app_records`). Migration `0028_forms_app_ids_additive` filled them through `accounting_link` by the kind each column holds, in the roster's company, and stops on any row with no record of that kind, or evidence of another company not marked as an earlier company's; `0029_forms_app_ids_finish` drops the request's QuickBooks columns and keeps every column of the evidence (`tests/integration/migrate-forms.test.ts`). Consents, copies, reminder claims and rounds and withdrawals hold no family id. In the code they are `AppFamilyId`, `AppStudentId` and `SubjectId` (`subjectOfRequest()`, `requestRecords()` in `src/lib/forms/requests.ts`); new audit rows name `familyId` and `studentId` in their body (older rows keep the QuickBooks ids, and the audit page's record filter finds both: `formRowsOf()` in `src/lib/audit/query.ts`, never for a kept form's rows). Documenso is sent what it was: the request's own id as `externalId`. A guardian's membership is `guardian.family_id` (`familyGuardians()`, `signedWithin()`, the copies and reminders). Staff pages and the reminders take the families from `loadSeasonFamilies()` (`docs/agents/seasons.md`); "Remind" for a family sends the app family's id (a uuid, `NOT_FROM_PAGE` otherwise). The evidence job stores the request's records and, as the evidence's copies, the customers their links name then (`namesOf()`, read in the store's own transaction after it takes the roster lock shared and before it locks the request row, since stage 3's slice 9d: every write of a link holds the roster lock exclusively, so a link made at that moment is never missed or half-seen), each null for a record with no link, and the company only with the family's copy (null when the family has no link or the roster names no company); a link made later fills nothing in. A move changes a kept form's `family_id` only. A roster read follows no move made in QuickBooks (stage 3's slice 9c); a move staff make takes a complete form to the new family's record at once, and its volunteer notices go only to guardians whose links are open.
- One `signature_request` per form requirement and subject (family or student); its id is the document's `externalId`. Its state is what Documenso's API last said, never what a webhook said: not opened, waiting, signed (waiting for the sealed copy), complete (COMPLETED, the only state that meets it), declined. `readAndStore()` (`src/lib/forms/requests.ts`) is the only way a state changes; the page, the webhook (`/api/documenso/webhook`: secret checked in constant time before the body is read, the message only a hint) and the worker's `forms.check` job (each open request read more than 5 minutes ago; asks Documenso to seal again after 20 minutes) all go through it, and each change is audited. A request an open holds (`preparing_at`, `isHeld()`) is left alone by every reader until the open has sent its document: Documenso reads it as a draft until then. A document that seems gone (404, or another `externalId`) resets only an unsigned request; one that holds a signature (`holdsSignature()`) is kept and asked about again, the 404 counts as a failure on the Health page, and an open that finds such a document of the request by `externalId` takes it up again (`form.document.recover`) rather than refusing. Each hand-out of a signing frame (`form.sign.open`) and each signature the portal first reads (`form.signer.signed`) is audited with the guardian it belongs to (spec §9.4).
- A parent finds a form only through `findMyForm(ctx, id)` and `loadMyForms()` (`src/lib/forms/my-forms.ts`): within the session's family (`ctx.familyId`, only while its record is active: `activeFamilyOf()`), with no link needed, and the forms its current season asks for. Nothing is made in Documenso and no signing token is read until the signed-in guardian has agreed to the consent text of the current `CONSENT_VERSION` (`src/lib/forms/consent.ts`; `signature_consent` records who, when, the address and the version). The signing token is never stored: it is read from Documenso at each open and given only to the guardian whose recipient it is. The frame is embedded directly; its "completed" message only makes the page read the state again.
- A completed request sets `evidence_owed`; the worker (`runSignedFormsJobs()`, `src/lib/signed-forms/worker.ts`) fetches its evidence a few per wake: every item's sealed PDF, the certificate PDF, the audit log as PDF and as JSON, and the SHA-256 of each sealed and original PDF, kept in `signed_form_evidence` and `signed_form_file` with the names, signers, consents, template and company as they were, in one transaction with `forms.evidence.stored`, and only then is `evidence_owed` cleared. A failure keeps nothing and backs off (5 minutes, 30, then every 3 hours). A family or student with no QuickBooks link is no reason to wait: its evidence is kept with no copy. The one state that waits is QuickBooks connected to a company other than the roster's, where every lookup through a link answers nothing though the links exist (`recordsCompany()` null while the roster names a company), so "no link" can't be told from "can't look": before fetching anything the job checks for it (`linksReadable()`) and asks Documenso nothing; it checks again around the lookups when storing (the files are fetched by then) and keeps nothing; either way it records the request's error as `other_company`, which the Health page's forms line says in words pointing at QuickBooks, not Documenso (`evidenceHealth().otherCompany`; nothing reaches it until stage 4, since connecting another company is refused; a request still holding the old `no_link` is tried again on its own schedule); a request that moved to another family while its files were fetched is `moved_while_fetched` and is read again next wake (`EvidenceNotStored`). A later fetch with a different hash keeps the first and audits `forms.evidence.mismatch` (shown on the Health page). Evidence has no foreign key to its request and is never deleted (`KEPT_TABLES`); since stage 4's slice M7a a change of company leaves it as it is, its family's (the families are kept), whatever company copy it holds; before that slice a change of company marked it `earlier_company_at` and cut it from its request and its app records, and such a row stays so: shown to no parent, left out of the matrix, listed in the staff Forms page's "Earlier company" archive (which holds only those), never matched to a family by a QuickBooks id alone. Each guardian who signed gets one copy by email (attached under 3 MB, a link otherwise: the form's page for one of several parts). A parent downloads their own at `/forms/[id]/signed`: a route handler that calls `requireParentPage()` first, looks the request up within the family (`src/lib/forms/my-evidence.ts`), answers the same 404 to anything else, and sends no-store as `signed-form.pdf`; evidence is matched by its request, the app family it names and as not archived (`currentEvidence()`, `src/lib/forms/evidence-company.ts`: `earlier_company_at` null, whatever its `realm_id`, so a family kept across a change of company keeps its forms; never an archived one). The staff Forms pages use the same rule: evidence with no company, or with a company the roster no longer names, is among the current forms, never in the "Earlier company" archive, and a change of company archives nothing (it is never deleted). A complete form goes with a moved student (it still counts), but its PDF holds the old family's guardians: a parent sees who signed a form, and gets its PDF, only when every guardian on it is a guardian of their family (`signedWithin()`, `src/lib/forms/requests.ts`); otherwise they read "Signed" alone. Staff: the matrix `/staff/forms` (`Forms.Signature.Read`, not audited; a form with no template reads "Not ready yet", its own cell state and filter in `src/lib/signed-forms/rules.ts`, and a family whose only outstanding forms have none is offered no Remind; below it the company's other kept signed forms, an earlier season's or a departed student's, then the earlier company's), a request's and an evidence's page (audited `forms.request.read`, `forms.evidence.read` before anything is shown), and downloads at `/staff/forms/evidence/[id]/[file]` (a route handler that checks the session and permission first and audits `forms.evidence.download` before a byte is sent). "Remind now", the reminder schedule (`requirement.form_reminders`) and "Start again" (the document read from Documenso first, never withdrawn once complete, then withdrawn as the verification says, the reason kept for staff) take `Forms.Signature.ReadWrite`. A guardian who can no longer sign for a family keeps a working signing link on any document made for them, so blocking a person, and every membership closed (`closeMembership()`, `src/lib/parents/membership.ts`: the row, the person's sessions, those forms and `parent.access.ended`, in the caller's transaction; its one caller since the reconcile went is staff's "remove a guardian", `removeGuardian()` in `src/lib/parents/guardians.ts`; an open under way when either commits stores nothing for them, `goneSigners()` in `src/lib/forms/signing.ts`), starts again in the same transaction the family's unfinished forms still waiting for that guardian's signature (`formsForGoneSigner()`, `src/lib/forms/signer-gone.ts`, audited `forms.request.signer_gone`); a form they signed already is left as it is. The worker withdraws those documents and a moved student's (`form_withdrawal`) the same way: read first, a completed one left in Documenso (`form.document.withdraw.kept`), one that keeps failing audited as `form.document.withdraw.failed`, never as done. Opens are rate-limited (`form_open_parent`). Tests: `tests/helpers/documenso-stub.ts` (`startDocumensoStub()`, behaving as the verification found: a role left out stays a recipient until deleted, search by `externalId` matches substrings, both email settings recorded) and `tests/helpers/forms.ts` (`connectForms()`, `seedFormRequirement()`, `seedRequest()`, `seedConsent()`, `seedEvidence()`, `fakePdf()`; the seeds take families and students by QuickBooks id and store the app's records, `familyRecord()` and `studentRecord()`, so a test that seeds one seeds a roster company first; `requestCustomers()`, `requestByCustomer()` and `groupOf()` read a request or a Forms page's group back by customer). The browser seed never sets Documenso up, so the e2e run can't reach one; its two requests and kept evidence are stored by the app's records after 0023's fill (`scripts/seed-e2e.ts`).
