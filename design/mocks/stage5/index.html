Turn off QuickBooks Online?

Families stop seeing invoices and Pay, and the portal stops sending changes to QuickBooks. Families, students, forms and volunteering stay as they are.

QuickBooks is disconnected. Its links are kept, so turning it on again with the same company picks up where it left off.
