# The Boosters Rocket name and logos

The code in this repository is open source under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE). You may
use, study, change and share it under that license.

The name **Boosters Rocket**, the wordmark `boostersrocket`, the rocket mark ("Afterburn") and the other files in
[`brand/logos/`](brand/logos/) are not covered by that license. They are reserved: they say where the software comes
from, and a family reading "Powered by Boosters Rocket" should be able to trust that it is this software.

## What you may do

- **Run it unmodified, as your club's portal.** A club running Boosters Rocket as published (a release, or this
  repository's code unchanged, with its own club name, logo and color set in the portal's settings) may show
  "Powered by Boosters Rocket" and the badge the portal draws, and may say in its own words that its portal runs on
  Boosters Rocket. Setting the club's name, logo, color and other settings the portal offers is not changing the
  software.
- **Talk about it.** You may use the name to refer to the project truthfully: in an article, a review, a talk, a
  comparison, or a note that your own software is based on it ("based on Boosters Rocket").
- **Contribute.** Changes offered back to this repository may use the name and logos as the project does.

## What needs your own name

- **A fork that changes the software** must use its own name and its own logo, and must not call itself Boosters
  Rocket or use the logos as its own, in the app, its emails, its store listings or its website. Take out or replace
  the "Powered by Boosters Rocket" badge and mail line, the setup pages' mark and the product's name in the app.
  "Based on Boosters Rocket" is fine.
- **A hosted service** offered to other clubs, changed or not, needs the project's written permission to use the name
  or logos, so families are not led to think the project runs it.
- **Merchandise, domain names and social accounts** using the name or the logos need the same written permission.

The brand guide, with how the logos may be drawn, is [`brand/BRAND.md`](brand/BRAND.md). Questions about using the
name or logos: open an issue in this repository.
