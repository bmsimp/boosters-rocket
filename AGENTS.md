## Boosters Rocket: rules for agents

Boosters Rocket is an open-source, self-hosted parent portal for booster clubs: each family signs in and sees only its own students, what it owes and what it must sign, while staff manage the roster. QuickBooks Online holds the club's books; the portal reads them and keeps a cache, which decides whose invoices a family sees. Families, students, guardians and sign-ins are the portal's own records, and it writes back to QuickBooks only what someone asks it to (`docs/agents/quickbooks-writes.md`).

**Where the truth lives**
- `docs/agents/owner-taste.md`: what the owner wants, in his words: how pages, actions, words and looks should be. Read it before touching any screen or copy; a design reviewer checks every PR against it.
- `PRODUCT.md`: users, principles, terms, voice. The visual system: `src/app/booster.css` (tokens and every class), the primitives in `src/app/components/ui/`, their index `docs/agents/ui.md`, and the gallery at `/staff/settings/design-system` (static twin `design/system/gallery.html`). The look is Material 3 Expressive (the owner's choice of 2026-11-26, his decisions D1 to D8 in `docs/agents/ui.md`). There is no `DESIGN.md`: it described the first look and was retired on 2026-11-26; the stylesheet, `docs/agents/ui.md`, the gallery and `docs/agents/owner-taste.md` are the whole of it.
- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-booster-rocket-v1-design.md`: the v1 spec. Later specs in that folder override the parts they name, and win where they disagree: `2026-11-10-parent-email-code-sign-in-design.md` (parents sign in with the portal's own emailed code, not Entra External ID), `2026-11-10-quickbooks-connection-and-roster-design.md`, `2026-11-11-parents-from-quickbooks-design.md` (step 5 changes its "What staff cannot do here": staff now create, rename, move and make inactive in QuickBooks from the roster pages), `2026-11-15-documenso-verification.md` (forms), `2026-11-24-connect-through-boosters-rocket-design.md` (QuickBooks connects through Boosters Rocket Connect, the relay, by default, or with the club's own Intuit app; a relay disconnect needs the Admin's acknowledgement). `2026-11-18-app-owned-families-design.md` is approved and being built in stages (the roadmap says which have shipped): the app is to own families, students, guardians and sign-ins, with the accounting system linked through a mapping screen. `2026-11-28-accounting-plugin-design.md` (stage 5, approved 2026-11-28, not yet built) overrides that spec's section 7: accounting becomes a plugin the core stands without (`src/plugins/`), and with none on an Admin chooses to track payments by hand or not at all. `2026-11-28-email-templates-design.md` (approved 2026-11-28): staff with `Settings.Emails.ReadWrite` change the words of the emails the portal sends, around fixed parts no one can change. Until a stage ships, the rules below describe the code as it is; the slice that changes the code changes the rule in the same commit.
- `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-07-v1-roadmap.md`: what is built and what is next. Read it for current status.
- `docs/conformance.md`: the v1 spec requirement by requirement, as overridden, each with its state and the file and test that prove it (step 7). A change that builds, changes or drops a requirement changes its row. `docs/walkthrough.md`: every journey walked in a browser at 390 and 1440 wide, with what was found. `docs/runbooks/before-the-first-family.md`: every check only a person can make, in the owner's order; a check that code can now make leaves it.

**How work is done**
- One screen-sized slice at a time, from a short brief. Tests are written with the code. No long plans with code in them.
- Do the slice in the brief and nothing else. Say in your report what you left undone, and anything that surprised you.
- A PR that touches a screen or copy runs the design-review agent (`.claude/agents/design-review.md`) on itself and fixes its findings before it asks the owner; its verdict and any findings left go in the PR body.
- From finished slice to open pull request with green CI: `.claude/skills/ship-pr/SKILL.md` (`/ship-pr`). It never merges or deploys.

### Privacy rules that are never traded
- The family comes only from the session: `ctx.familyId` (the app's own family record, `AppFamilyId`, which the session stores as that record's id, `parent_auth_session.family_id`) from `requireParentPage()` or `parentAction`. An id from the URL, a form or an action argument is untrusted: look it up within that family, and answer `notFound()` or refuse when it isn't there. A student's id is the app's, the same in every family: a lookup by a student's id is always joined to the family (`studentsStillIn()`).
- Staff-only query modules are never imported by parent code: `src/lib/roster/queries.ts` (`loadRosterPage`, `rosterFamilies`, `rosterEntry` load every family); in `src/lib/parents/directory.ts`, `listPeople`, `getPerson`, `familySignins`, `personContact`, `familyContact` (the note at the top of the file); `src/lib/seasons/setup.ts`, `subjects.ts`, `staff-progress.ts`, `due-reminders.ts` and `everyone.ts` (the season's reads for every family at once: every chosen list, tick and waiver; the parent-safe reads in `recorded.ts`, `season-rows.ts` and `src/lib/families/links.ts` each take the families and students they are for, and `src/lib/families/session-family.ts` the one family it is asked about); every module of `src/lib/signed-forms/` (the matrix, evidence, reminders, start again: they read many families); `src/lib/volunteer/setup.ts`, `staff-credit.ts`, `reminders.ts` and `moved-student.ts`; `src/lib/buyouts/staff.ts`, `daily-check.ts`, `setup.ts`, `moved.ts` and `close.ts`; `src/lib/staff-home/today.ts` (the staff home's counts); `src/lib/roster/writes.ts` and `queue.ts`; `src/lib/quickbooks/sandbox-check/run.ts` and `writes.ts`; and `src/lib/families/roster-import.ts` (the import of the app's own records from the roster, `docs/agents/app-records.md`); and `src/lib/roster/no-family.ts` (the customers no family is linked to, and "Create a family from this": it reads every customer of the roster cache); and `src/lib/parents/guardians.ts` (staff add, remove and reinstate guardians: it gives any address a sign-in for any family); and `src/lib/families/guardian-events.ts` (a guardian's save's event, `records.events`, stage 5's slice P6: its handler queues writes for any guardian row of any family); and `src/lib/parents/people.ts` (staff block and rename people: a rename starts sends to QuickBooks, stage 3's slice 9h); and `src/lib/parents/contact-take-in.ts` (the take-in of contact details, stage 3's slice 11a: it reads every person, guardian row, family and cached customer); and `src/lib/accounting/mapping.ts` (the accounting links screen, stage 4's slice M1: it reads every family's guardians' sign-in addresses and every customer of the roster cache); and `src/lib/accounting/link-writes.ts` (its presses, stage 4's slice M3a: they link any family to any customer, reading every family's guardians and every customer of the roster cache to check it); and `src/lib/parents/signin-reset.ts` (an Admin's reset of a parent's sign-in, the sign-in release's slice SI-9a: it reads and removes any person's sign-in, and the staff home's line reads every recent reset); and `src/lib/accounting/guardian-links.ts` (its guardian presses, stage 4's slice M3c: they link any guardian row to any Parent customer and end any row's link, reading every family's guardians and every customer of the roster cache to check it); and `src/lib/accounting/link-details.ts` (Show details on Accounting links, the redesign of 2026-11-27: any family's guardians' sign-in addresses and phones beside its customer's in the roster cache, audited as a read of one family); and `src/lib/accounting/ended-links.ts` (stage 4's slice M3b2: the links an unlink ended whose customer is still in the books, read for any family, student and guardian row with the roster cache); and `src/lib/quickbooks/switch.ts` (the switch page's counts, stage 4's slice M8: every family's links, records, requests and buy-outs); and `src/lib/quickbooks/relay.ts` (the signed calls to Boosters Rocket Connect: they open the portal's private key and carry the club's tokens); and `src/lib/quickbooks/relay-registration.ts` (the portal's registration with it: it makes and seals that key and asks the relay about it); and `src/lib/accounting/import-writes.ts` (the reviewed import's press, stage 4's slice M6: it makes families of any customers no family is linked to and gives their addresses a family's sign-in, reading every customer of the roster cache, every family's guardians and the persons who hold the proposed addresses); and `src/lib/messages/staff.ts` (club messages for staff, slices 2 and 4: every message, staff members' held files, posts, edits and an Admin's delete) and `src/lib/messages/mail.ts` (their mail, slice 4: every parent's sign-in address, and the failed addresses staff see); `src/lib/backups/download.ts`, `requests.ts` and `offsite.ts` (in-app backups: a backup holds every family's records, and off-site copies' sealed key and passphrase) are staff only too. `src/lib/messages/body.ts`, the body's check and drawing for a page and a mail, is parent code, in `PARENT_CODE_ELSEWHERE`. `src/lib/parents/contact-queue.ts` (stage 3's slice 11b: a parent's change of their details queued in the save's transaction, and since slice S11e their confirmed change of sign-in email in the confirm's; since stage 5's slice P6 reached through the save's event, `src/lib/families/events.ts`, parent code too) is parent code, in `PARENT_CODE_ELSEWHERE`, and imports none of them, nor the QuickBooks client. They are `STAFF_ONLY_IMPORTS` in `tests/integration/page-guards.test.ts`. A parent page reads through a query that takes the family id and returns that family alone.
- A note staff write about a family is for staff: a waiver's reason is in the audit log and on staff pages, and a parent reads only "Waived"; a volunteer credit's note or a removed check-in's reason likewise (a parent reads "Added by the club"), and a form's start-again reason is only in its audit row. `seedTwoFamilies()` gives each family such a note (`season.staffOnly`), and `tests/integration/parent-isolation.test.ts` fails a parent page that shows one, even the family's own.
- Anything stored against a QuickBooks id means another customer in another company. Since stage 4's slice M8 a different company is connected only after an Admin asks for it, on the switch page (`/staff/settings/quickbooks/switch`) or in step 2 of setup ("A different company", the owner's finding of 2026-11-25), both through `beginSwitchConnect()`: without that ask, connecting one is refused (`completeConnect()`, `src/lib/quickbooks/connection.ts`, reason `roster_company`, in words on the QuickBooks setup page), and a database connected to another company with no switch recorded never has that company's roster read over this one's (`refreshRoster()`, a failed run `roster_company`, asking QuickBooks nothing; its entry is in `docs/runbooks/health-warnings.md`); reconnecting the same company works as before. How a switch reaches the clear (the ask, the store, step 3b, the refresh's backstop) is in `docs/agents/app-records.md`. The clear a change of company runs, and what it keeps, is in `docs/agents/app-records.md` ("A change of company"). A new table keyed by a QuickBooks id is cleared there too.
- QuickBooks text (names, emails, memos) is rendered as text only: never `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, never built into HTML, a URL or CSS.
- Pass a client component only what it shows: every prop is sent to the browser, rendered or not.
- Nothing family-specific is ever cached in a service worker or a shared cache. Route handlers that return data send `cache-control: no-store` (the signed-form downloads too, with a fixed file name: QuickBooks text never goes into a header). The one exception is the club's app icons (`src/app/icons/[name]/route.ts`): public, the same for everyone, and kept by their `?v=` version.
- The service worker (`public/sw.js`) stores one thing, `/offline.html`, and does one thing: it answers a page navigation that failed for lack of a connection with that page. It has one `cache.put`, no `add` or `addAll`, and no `respondWith` for anything but `mode === "navigate"`; it never stores a page, the data a page loads, an API answer or a script. Never add a caching strategy to it. `tests/unit/pwa-static.test.ts` reads the file for this, and `tests/e2e/pwa.spec.ts` signs a parent in, browses, and checks the caches hold `/offline.html` and nothing else. `public/offline.html` is the same for every club and person: no club name, no family data, no script.
- No secret reaches the browser: `scripts/check-client-bundle.mjs` searches the built client files for the secret values. Never read `process.env` or `@/env` in a client component.

### Checklists
New parent page (`src/app/(parent)/**/page.tsx`; example `src/app/(parent)/payments/page.tsx`):
- Make the first line of the page `const ctx = await requireParentPage("/its-address");`: the page's own address, written out as a string, so a visitor who has to sign in or choose a family first is brought back to it. A dynamic segment passes a template that starts with the fixed part: `` requireParentPage(`/forms/${id}`) ``. Only Home (`/`) calls it with no argument. `tests/integration/page-guards.test.ts` refuses a missing address, another page's, or one it can't read; `tests/integration/parent-isolation.test.ts` checks the redirect carries it.
- The parent layout is not a guard: it calls `parentLayoutCtx()` (`src/lib/guards.ts`), draws the frame for a signed-in parent and never redirects. Do not put `requireParentPage()` in a layout: its redirect runs before the page's and cannot name the page.
- The return path travels through the browser, so it is checked at every read by `safeReturnPath()` (`src/lib/auth/return-path.ts`): only a plain parent path on this site gets through, and anything else lands on `/`. Never redirect to a `next` value that has not been through it. A new top-level folder in `src/app` outside `(parent)` that holds a page or a route handler is added to `NOT_PARENT_SECTIONS` there (`tests/unit/return-path.test.ts` fails until it is).
- Read data with `ctx.familyId` only. Do not audit a parent viewing their own family.
- A page that gets a tab: add it to `PARENT_NAV` (`src/app/components/parent-nav.ts`; its icon goes in `parent-tabs.tsx`) and to the expected list in `tests/unit/parent-nav.test.ts`, and take it out of the 404 list in `tests/e2e/public-pages.spec.ts`.
- A query module for parent pages outside `src/app/(parent)/`: list it in `PARENT_CODE_ELSEWHERE` (a file) or `PARENT_FACING_LIB` (a folder) in `tests/integration/page-guards.test.ts`, so the staff-only import scan reads it.
- A dynamic segment: add its params to `PARAMS` in both `tests/integration/parent-isolation.test.ts` and `tests/e2e/parent-params.ts` (there with ids from `scripts/seed-e2e.ts`; `parent-session.spec.ts` and `parent-a11y.spec.ts` both read it). Each fails until it has them.
- New kinds of family data: seed them for both families in `seedTwoFamilies()` and add their strings to the markers.
- New `scripts/seed-e2e.ts` data for any family but `E2E-FAMILY`: add its strings to `OTHER_FAMILIES` in `tests/e2e/parent-session.spec.ts`, the only test that reads the props sent to client components.

Nothing else makes a parent session, sets its cookie or calls Better Auth's sign-in or sign-up (`tests/unit/one-way-in.test.ts`). A new way in hands the finish the proof of what it spent, which `signinStands()` reads again under the person's lock.
- The action is `"use server"` in `src/app/sign-in/password-actions.ts` only, exporting only `beforeSignInAction` functions (`src/lib/guards.ts`, the door `"before-sign-in"`, which `tests/integration/invariants.test.ts` allows in that one file: another file is a decision named in a brief). Check every argument's type and size, and refuse anything else as a bad request.
- Every refusal gets one answer, the same words, and as near as possible the same reads and time, whatever the reason (unknown, used, expired, blocked, no row that counts, sign-ins off, over a limit: an over-limit refusal still reads the link and throws the answer away; it differs in being audited once per full window and with no person, and, when the address is over its limit, in counting nothing for the link); the reason goes only in its audit row. The one exception: an over-limit answer may say so in words of its own (the password step's `TOO_MANY_TRIES`), but only where the count is taken before anything is read and by what the visitor sent (the address typed, an IP address, a token, a device cookie only its own device's row answers to), so being over it is the same for an address on file and one that isn't. Limit it per IP address first (skipped when the address is unknown, as `takeIpLimit()` in `signin-codes.ts`) and then per token, counted under the token's keyed hash, never the token. Do slow outside work (the breach check) and the password hash outside any transaction, then check again under the person's lock and write, with the audit row, in one transaction.
- The page is `export const dynamic = "force-dynamic"`, so it is sent `no-store`; a page whose address carries a token is sent `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` as a header, by its own rule in `next.config.ts` after the site's (`LINK_PAGES` there; `tests/unit/pwa-static.test.ts` pins it), so not even the head's first requests carry the token, and also sets `referrer: "no-referrer"` in its metadata (streamed into the body, since the root layout's metadata is async), and loads nothing from outside. It shows nothing about a person or a family (no name, no address, no username for a password manager), and its client form is given only what is already in its address.

Rules for all of them:
- A staff member acting on their own record only (their name) uses `staffSelfAction` (`src/lib/guards.ts`): no permission, and the action takes no id, only `ctx.staffUserId`.
- Audit: the audit page's record filter, and how it finds a record's rows written before an area moved to the app's ids, is in `docs/agents/audit.md`.
- Audit: `writeAudit` in the same transaction as every write. A staff read of one family's or person's data is audited. A parent viewing their own family is not. Write the action as a string (`action: "area.thing.verb"`) and give it words in `ACTION_NAMES` (`src/lib/audit/actions.ts`), which the audit page and its export show: `tests/unit/audit-actions.test.ts` fails for an action with none.
- Permissions are in `src/lib/permissions/registry.ts`. `ADMIN_ONLY` ones can't be given to a custom role by any pattern (`tests/unit/permissions/rules.test.ts` tries every form); the audit log (`Audit.Log.Read`, its rows hold staff-only notes) and the Health page (`Admin.Health.Read`) are among them. A role takes any other permission by its exact name. Every permission has plain words in `PERMISSION_WORDS` and an area in `PERMISSION_AREAS` (`src/lib/permissions/words.ts`; `tests/unit/permission-words.test.ts` fails without them). Roles are edited as a checklist and saved as exact names (`rulesFromChecklist()`, which adds the "see" permission a "change" one brings: `readTwinOf()`, Roster.Read for `Roster.Family.ReadWrite` and for `Accounting.Link.ReadWrite`); the linking permission ("Link families to accounting", open question 1 of the app-owned families spec) is not Admin-only: an Admin holds it and a custom role such as a treasurer's may be given it; a role written with patterns is shown with what it grants today, plus what a save would add, said in a note.
- Action result: `{ ok: true, message }` or `{ ok: false, errors }` (`errors` is a list of sentences to show). Client forms call actions through `safely()` from `src/app/components/action-error.ts`.
- A guard refusal says the same thing whatever the reason. Never tell a parent why, or that another family exists.

### Tests

- Commands: `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` (vitest, unit and integration against a real Postgres), `pnpm vitest run <files>`, `pnpm e2e` (Playwright). The repository variable `CI_RUNNER_LABELS` (a JSON list of runner labels) is unset here; it stays available to a fork, or a club with a private repository, that wants its own runner (`ci/runner/`). The club's own runner was retired on 2026-11-23. Never let a self-hosted runner serve a public repository: a pull request from a fork would run its code on that machine. Fork pull requests also need the owner's approval before workflows run. Dependabot's pull requests always run on hosted runners, whatever the variable says (`runs-on` in the workflows): they bring third-party code, and a job on a club's own runner can reach a privileged Docker daemon.
- A focused run (`.github/workflows/focused.yml`, the owner's approval of 2026-11-20): by hand only (`workflow_dispatch`, never a push or a pull request), on the ref it is dispatched on, it runs `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm lint`, then `pnpm vitest run` on the files named, then, when specs are named, builds, seeds and runs only those Playwright specs, on the runner, Postgres service and env of CI's test job (its Postgres image is held to the others by `tests/unit/postgres-version.test.ts`). `gh workflow run focused.yml --ref <branch> -f vitest="tests/unit/a.test.ts tests/integration/b.test.ts" -f e2e=""`, or `scripts/focused-run.sh <branch> [--e2e "<specs>"] <vitest files...>`, which dispatches, finds its run, watches it and prints the failed lines (it pushes nothing). Each path is checked in the job (`tests/unit/` or `tests/integration/` ending `.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/` ending `.spec.ts`, no `..`, present on the branch; at most 40 in all; not both inputs empty). For it a builder or fixer may push a work-in-progress branch (the owner allowed those pushes for this), deleted once the slice's pull request is opened. One run at a time on the runner: a second dispatch on the same ref cancels the first. A run whose specs saved pictures under `test-results/screenshots/` (`tests/e2e/screenshots.spec.ts`: the QuickBooks settings page, setup's step 2 and the switch page at 390 and 1440) uploads them as the artifact `screenshots`, kept a week: `gh run download <run id> -n screenshots`. It never replaces the pull request's full CI run.
- The full suite runs its files in parallel: unit files beside integration files, each integration file on its worker's own database. `BR_TEST_WORKERS` sets how many workers (default half the cores, at most 6; any number from 1 to 16). `BR_TEST_WORKERS=1` runs the files one at a time on the run's database, as the suite once did. About 200 files take 5 minutes on this machine when it is quiet and up to 30 when other agents are testing. Still run it in the background and read its output, and never run two at once: a second run on the same database refuses to start. If a file you did not touch fails only with a timeout, run that file once on its own before you look further. While working, run the files you touched plus `tests/integration/invariants.test.ts`, `tests/integration/page-guards.test.ts` and `tests/integration/parent-isolation.test.ts`. Run the full suite before you finish.
- Postgres is the container `booster-rocket-db-1` on `127.0.0.1:5433` (Postgres 18; user and password `booster`). The default test database is `booster_test`; the tests empty it again and again (`resetDb()` in `tests/helpers/db.ts`), so never point them at data you want.
- Moving the local database to Postgres 18: after pulling the change that moved it, `docker compose up -d` leaves the database stopped, and `docker compose logs db` says why. Stop every test run on this machine first (the move stops the database they share), then from the main tree run `scripts/move-postgres.sh dev ~/booster-rocket-moving-to-postgres-18` and `docker compose up -d`. The database `booster` is kept, every row checked; the test databases are not carried (`booster_test` comes back empty from `docker/initdb`; make private ones again). The old data stays in the volume `booster-rocket_pgdata`: going back is `scripts/move-postgres.sh dev --stop`, checking out an older commit and `docker compose up -d`; coming forward again is the move with `--again` (it backs up what 18 holds into the folder first, and asks for `--discard-18` when 18 holds work done since the move). The dev move refuses in a checkout that has `deploy/.env` or `.env.poc`. Remove it only when sure: `docker compose down` (never `-v`), then `docker volume rm booster-rocket_pgdata`.

### UI
- Follow Material Design 3 in its Expressive style (`docs/agents/owner-taste.md` rule 7) through our own tokens and primitives: use only the tokens and classes in `src/app/booster.css` and the primitives in `src/app/components/ui` (`docs/agents/ui.md`). A fixed color added to the stylesheet is added to `src/lib/theme/palette.ts` too, where `tests/unit/design-tokens.test.ts` checks its contrast, with every club role's for a set of seeds and the focus ring (3px in primary, or on-surface where primary can't hold 3:1: D8).
- Font sizes in rem, never px. Every touch target at least 48 by 48 (M3's minimum: a button drawn 40px sits in a 48px target; a page's main button and sheet and dialog footers are 56px: D4). Every status is words, plus an icon where there is room; color never carries meaning alone. Expressive never costs readability: body text 1rem, labels above fields (never floating), and text never moves while it is read; springs move containers and shapes only, and reduced motion moves nothing.
- Buttons are M3's set (filled for the one thing an area is for, tonal, outlined, text, danger), fully round; status labels have 8px corners so they never look like buttons (D7). An action's answer is `ActionOutcome` drawn in place, taking the focus, never a snackbar. Data tables, notices and status labels are ours, on M3's tokens.
- Show an instant with `LocalTime` and a calendar date with `CalendarDate` (`src/app/components/`), not with your own formatting. Show an amount with `formatMoney()`.
- Staff pages, Settings and parent pages use the same primitives (`docs/agents/ui.md`): `DataTable`, `Notice` and `ActionOutcome`, the `.field` and `.checks` controls (`TextInput`, `Checkbox`) and `ChoiceGroup`. Font sizes never under 0.8125rem, body text 1rem. A parent page's busy button is `aria-disabled`, never `disabled` (a disabled button drops the focus). A link alone on its line, or on a line made only of links, takes `.link-line` (48px). Every club bar that has links starts with "Skip to the page" (`ClubBar`), drawn only while focused, landing on `PageStart` past the bar and the Switch family line (`ParentFrame`, the staff layout); a page's `<main>` needn't change. An account link's accessible name starts with the initials it shows. `env(safe-area-inset-*)` for the top and sides appears only in that last block.
- Anything that starts or changes something, or takes the person to the next step of a task, is a button (the owner's rule of 2026-11-25): `Button` for a form's submit, `LinkButton` for going to an action or a step, primary for the one thing the page or step is for and secondary for the others. A link alone on its line still takes `.link-line`. `tests/unit/actions-as-buttons.test.ts` refuses a bare `<a>` or `<Link>` on a staff, parent or sign-in page whose written words start like an action or a step ("Next:", "Skip to", "Connect" and so on); words from a constant aren't seen, so it is a floor.
- No fold-outs (the owner's rule of 2026-11-25): no `<details>`/`<summary>` anywhere in `src/app`, and no disclosure primitive (`tests/unit/no-fold-outs.test.ts`), except one the owner approved on 2026-11-27: a button-driven row expansion that compares two records side by side (Accounting links' Show details: a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` whose words turn to Hide details, `DataTable`'s `expand`), holding no action but the way to its row's Drawer. A button's label is a verb and its object saying exactly what happens; a consequence a person must know before pressing is one short sentence by the button; an explanation is one or two sentences at most, and longer help is left out of the app (`docs/agents/ui.md`).
