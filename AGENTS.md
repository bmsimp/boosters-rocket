## Booster Rocket: rules for agents

Booster Rocket is an open-source, self-hosted parent portal for booster clubs: each family signs in and sees only its own students, what it owes and what it must sign, while staff manage the roster. QuickBooks Online holds the club's books; the portal reads them and keeps a cache, which decides whose invoices each family sees, while the families, students and sign-ins are the portal's own records (since stage 3's slice 9c nothing read from QuickBooks changes them; a family customer added in QuickBooks comes into the portal only when a staff member who may link families to accounting makes a family from it on Reconciliation; and since slice 9d a family staff add is the portal's own record first, made in QuickBooks too only by staff who may link families to accounting, at once or later with "Create in QuickBooks and link", and a family with no link is changed in the portal only), and it writes back only what someone asks it to: a guardian's phone and mailing address (changed by the parent or by staff), the roster changes staff make (new families, students, parents and members, names, a student's family, making a customer inactive), and a volunteer buy-out's invoice, made when a guardian chooses to pay instead of volunteering and voided when they switch back (Writes to QuickBooks, below). Since stage 3's slice 11d a parent's sign-in email is changed in the portal alone, and nothing sends it to QuickBooks until slice S11e, released with it. Since stage 3's slice 11a a guardian's phone and mailing address are the portal's own (The app's own records, below), and since slice 11b each change saved to them is sent on to their own record there: a parent's by the write queue's worker, and, since slice 11c, a staff member's tried at once after the save and otherwise left for the worker (Writes to QuickBooks, below).

### Privacy rules that are never traded
- The family comes only from the session: `ctx.familyId` (the app's own family record, `AppFamilyId`, which the session stores as that record's id, `parent_auth_session.family_id`) from `requireParentPage()` or `parentAction`. An id from the URL, a form or an action argument is untrusted: look it up within that family, and answer `notFound()` or refuse when it isn't there. A student's id is the app's, the same in every family: a lookup by a student's id is always joined to the family (`studentsStillIn()`).
- Staff-only query modules are never imported by parent code: `src/lib/roster/queries.ts` (`loadRosterPage`, `rosterFamilies`, `rosterEntry` load every family); in `src/lib/parents/directory.ts`, `listPeople`, `getPerson`, `familySignins`, `personContact`, `familyContact` (the note at the top of the file); `src/lib/seasons/setup.ts`, `subjects.ts`, `staff-progress.ts`, `due-reminders.ts` and `everyone.ts` (the season's reads for every family at once: every chosen list, tick and waiver; the parent-safe reads in `recorded.ts`, `season-rows.ts` and `src/lib/families/links.ts` each take the families and students they are for, and `src/lib/families/session-family.ts` the one family it is asked about); every module of `src/lib/signed-forms/` (the matrix, evidence, reminders, start again: they read many families); `src/lib/volunteer/setup.ts`, `staff-credit.ts`, `reminders.ts` and `moved-student.ts`; `src/lib/buyouts/staff.ts`, `daily-check.ts`, `setup.ts`, `moved.ts` and `close.ts`; `src/lib/staff-home/today.ts` (the staff home's counts); `src/lib/roster/writes.ts` and `queue.ts`; `src/lib/quickbooks/sandbox-check/run.ts` and `writes.ts`; and `src/lib/families/roster-import.ts` (the import of the app's own records from the roster, below); and `src/lib/roster/no-family.ts` (the customers no family is linked to, and "Create a family from this": it reads every customer of the roster cache); and `src/lib/parents/guardians.ts` (staff add, remove and reinstate guardians: it gives any address a sign-in for any family); and `src/lib/parents/people.ts` (staff block and rename people: a rename starts sends to QuickBooks, stage 3's slice 9h); and `src/lib/parents/contact-take-in.ts` (the take-in of contact details, stage 3's slice 11a: it reads every person, guardian row, family and cached customer); and `src/lib/accounting/mapping.ts` (the accounting links screen, stage 4's slice M1: it reads every family's guardians' sign-in addresses and every customer of the roster cache); and `src/lib/accounting/link-writes.ts` (its presses, stage 4's slice M3a: they link any family to any customer, reading every family's guardians and every customer of the roster cache to check it). `src/lib/parents/contact-queue.ts` (stage 3's slice 11b: a parent's change of their details queued in the save's transaction) is parent code, in `PARENT_CODE_ELSEWHERE`, and imports none of them, nor the QuickBooks client. They are `STAFF_ONLY_IMPORTS` in `tests/integration/page-guards.test.ts`. A parent page reads through a query that takes the family id and returns that family alone.
- A note staff write about a family is for staff: a waiver's reason is in the audit log and on staff pages, and a parent reads only "Waived"; a volunteer credit's note or a removed check-in's reason likewise (a parent reads "Added by the club"), and a form's start-again reason is only in its audit row. `seedTwoFamilies()` gives each family such a note (`season.staffOnly`), and `tests/integration/parent-isolation.test.ts` fails a parent page that shows one, even the family's own.
- Anything stored against a QuickBooks id means another customer in another company. From stage 2 of the app-owned families spec until stage 4 ships, a different company is refused (open question 18): connecting one (`completeConnect()`, `src/lib/quickbooks/connection.ts`, reason `roster_company`, in words on the QuickBooks setup page) and reading one's roster over this one's (`refreshRoster()`, a failed run `roster_company` that says to disconnect and connect the roster's company, asking QuickBooks nothing; its entry is in `docs/runbooks/health-warnings.md`) both stop while the roster cache names another company; reconnecting the same company works as before. So nothing the portal runs reaches the clear below (when the mapping screen turns that path back on, through connect and refresh, it needs its end-to-end tests again: until stage 2 they connected a different company and read its roster; they are refusal tests now); it is kept whole for the areas that have not moved yet (their part of `tests/integration/company-change-step5.test.ts` calls it directly), and its season, volunteering (buy-outs with it) and forms parts (`clearSeasonRecords()`, then `clearVolunteerRecords()`, then `clearFormRecords()`) and the money cache (`family_money`) and the write queue (both deleted after them) run first, because those records name the app's own families and students and the database refuses to remove a record one names. The clear, inside `clearRosterOfOtherCompanyLocked()` (`src/lib/roster/source.ts`): parent access (every guardian row closed, sign-ins and welcome emails switched off; since stage 3's slice 7a no snapshot of the old company is kept, and stage 4 decides what a change of company still does to sign-ins), `family_money`, the seasons' ticks, waivers, chosen lists and due-date reminder claims (`requirement_reminder`, `clearSeasonRecords()`), the item list and the money run's place (`clearMoneyRecords()`), every volunteer sign-up, credit, reminder claim and buy-out (`clearVolunteerRecords()`, which counts the buy-outs in its audit row), every signature request with its consents and the form reminder claims and rounds (`clearFormRecords()`), parents' sessions and email change requests (`clearParentAccess()`, the first part of the clear: both name the app's own family a session chose or a change was asked in, by `restrict` keys, so they go before `clearAppRecords()`), the write queue, waiting or finished (deleted before the app's records, since each item is filed under them, and counted as `writesDeleted` in `roster.cleared`), the QuickBooks check's own records (`qbo_check_record`, `clearCheckRecords()` in `src/lib/quickbooks/sandbox-check/view.ts`; its runs, which hold no customer id, are kept), and the app's own families, students and links with each guardian row's family (`clearAppRecords()`, `src/lib/families/company-change.ts`, audited `accounting.links.cleared`; since stage 3's slice 9c nothing makes them again by itself: no roster read imports records, and stage 4's import will). A new table keyed by a QuickBooks id is cleared there too. The one exception is a signed form's evidence: it is the club's record and is never deleted (Forms, below); the clear marks it as the earlier company's and cuts it from its request and from the app's family and student it named, in one statement, before those records go. `tests/integration/company-change-step5.test.ts` proves both (a money row of each family and a queued change filed under a family and student included), and that a different company is refused and every season, volunteering, buy-out, money and forms record kept, evidence and its files included.
- QuickBooks text (names, emails, memos) is rendered as text only: never `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, never built into HTML, a URL or CSS.
- Pass a client component only what it shows: every prop is sent to the browser, rendered or not.
- Nothing family-specific is ever cached in a service worker or a shared cache. Route handlers that return data send `cache-control: no-store` (the signed-form downloads too, with a fixed file name: QuickBooks text never goes into a header). The one exception is the club's app icons (`src/app/icons/[name]/route.ts`): public, the same for everyone, and kept by their `?v=` version.
- The service worker (`public/sw.js`) stores one thing, `/offline.html`, and does one thing: it answers a page navigation that failed for lack of a connection with that page. It has one `cache.put`, no `add` or `addAll`, and no `respondWith` for anything but `mode === "navigate"`; it never stores a page, the data a page loads, an API answer or a script. Never add a caching strategy to it. `tests/unit/pwa-static.test.ts` reads the file for this, and `tests/e2e/pwa.spec.ts` signs a parent in, browses, and checks the caches hold `/offline.html` and nothing else. `public/offline.html` is the same for every club and person: no club name, no family data, no script.
- No secret reaches the browser: `scripts/check-client-bundle.mjs` searches the built client files for the secret values. Never read `process.env` or `@/env` in a client component.

### The app's own records (stage 1 of the app-owned families spec)
- `family` and `student` hold the portal's own families and students, `guardian.family_id` each membership's family, and `accounting_link` the tie of each to its QuickBooks customer: `system` (`quickbooks`), `realm_id` (the company), `customer_id`, and exactly one of `family_id`, `student_id` or `guardian_id`, which is the link's kind. A customer may have one family record and one student record, each with its own link: within a company a customer has at most one link of each record kind, and each record one link. A membership link is unique by its guardian row only, since several rows may have come from one record. `synced_token` and `synced_at` (stage 3's slice 9e, migration 0041) are the customer's QuickBooks version the portal last aligned with: set from the answer of a send that carried the link's own version (or the one resend after a live read found the field as the portal held it, or "Send the portal's value"), from a create's answer (`changeOwnRecord()`), from the cache row of a change the queue found made by the same rule (`foundDone()`, `alignAfterWrite()`), and, for a link staff confirm ("Create a family from this", "Link the student to it") or the import makes, from the cache row of its customer; never from a roster read, a read-again, a shape save, a parent's write, a move or make-inactive sent with a page version other than the link's, or the family action's last step when the version it just read differs from the link's (one that equals it carried the link's own version, and its answer aligns the link by that same rule: an answer aligns a link only when what was sent carried the link's own version, or was a checked resend, `alignAfterWrite()`; ### Sign-in release
- The owner approved a new way for parents to sign in: a password chosen from a one-time set-up link, a second step on a new device, passkeys, an authenticator app, a club level of Standard or Strict, and an Admin-only reset; the emailed code stops being a way in by itself. - The lead's decisions for the whole release. The portal owns the password and everything but the passkey ceremony: Better Auth's `emailAndPassword` stays off on the parent side and no two-factor plugin is added; a password is hashed and checked with `hashPassword` and `verifyPassword` from `better-auth/crypto` and kept as the `credential` row of `parent_auth_account`; set-up and reset links, remembered devices, the second step and the authenticator app are the portal's own tables and code over `node:crypto`, so every such write shares the portal's transaction with its audit row. ### Forms (Documenso)
- Forms are signed in the club's own Documenso (v1 spec §9 as corrected by `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-documenso-verification.md`). `src/lib/documenso/client.ts` is its only client: server only, a timeout on every call, errors as short codes, nothing logged, no token in any message. Its address, API token and webhook secret are Forms settings (`/staff/settings/forms`, `Settings.Forms.ReadWrite`, Admin only), the secrets sealed; the address must be exactly the origin Documenso is configured with.
- Since stage 2 of the app-owned families spec a request names the app's own records, never a QuickBooks id: `signature_request.family_id` (never null) is the family whose request it is, and `student_id` the student a per-student form is signed for (null for a per-family form, whose subject is the family), each a `restrict` foreign key; one request per form and subject (`signature_request_family_uq`, `signature_request_student_uq`). The current company's evidence names them too (`signed_form_evidence.family_id`, `student_id`) beside its own copies of the QuickBooks ids (`family_qbo_id`, `subject_qbo_id`, `subject_kind`, `realm_id`), which are never changed, removed or used to find a family; an earlier company's names neither (`signed_form_evidence_app_records`). Migration `0028_forms_app_ids_additive` filled them through `accounting_link` by the kind each column holds, in the roster's company, and stops on any row with no record of that kind, or evidence of another company not marked as an earlier company's; `0029_forms_app_ids_finish` drops the request's QuickBooks columns and keeps every column of the evidence (`tests/integration/migrate-forms.test.ts`). Consents, copies, reminder claims and rounds and withdrawals hold no family id. In the code they are `AppFamilyId`, `AppStudentId` and `SubjectId` (`subjectOfRequest()`, `requestRecords()` in `src/lib/forms/requests.ts`); new audit rows name `familyId` and `studentId` in their body (older rows keep the QuickBooks ids, and the audit page's record filter finds both: `formRowsOf()` in `src/lib/audit/query.ts`, never for a kept form's rows). Documenso is sent what it was: the request's own id as `externalId`. A guardian's membership is `guardian.family_id` (`familyGuardians()`, `signedWithin()`, the copies and reminders). Staff pages and the reminders take the families from `loadSeasonFamilies()` (Seasons, below); "Remind" for a family sends the app family's id (a uuid, `NOT_FROM_PAGE` otherwise). The evidence job stores the request's records and, as the evidence's copies, the customers their links name then (`namesOf()`, read in the store's own transaction after it takes the roster lock shared and before it locks the request row, since stage 3's slice 9d: every write of a link holds the roster lock exclusively, so a link made at that moment is never missed or half-seen), each null for a record with no link, and the company only with the family's copy (null when the family has no link or the roster names no company); a link made later fills nothing in. A move changes a kept form's `family_id` only. A roster read follows no move made in QuickBooks (stage 3's slice 9c); a move staff make takes a complete form to the new family's record at once, and its volunteer notices go only to guardians whose links are open.
- One `signature_request` per form requirement and subject (family or student); its id is the document's `externalId`. Its state is what Documenso's API last said, never what a webhook said: not opened, waiting, signed (waiting for the sealed copy), complete (COMPLETED, the only state that meets it), declined. `readAndStore()` (`src/lib/forms/requests.ts`) is the only way a state changes; the page, the webhook (`/api/documenso/webhook`: secret checked in constant time before the body is read, the message only a hint) and the worker's `forms.check` job (each open request read more than 5 minutes ago; asks Documenso to seal again after 20 minutes) all go through it, and each change is audited. A request an open holds (`preparing_at`, `isHeld()`) is left alone by every reader until the open has sent its document: Documenso reads it as a draft until then. A document that seems gone (404, or another `externalId`) resets only an unsigned request; one that holds a signature (`holdsSignature()`) is kept and asked about again, the 404 counts as a failure on the Health page, and an open that finds such a document of the request by `externalId` takes it up again (`form.document.recover`) rather than refusing. Each hand-out of a signing frame (`form.sign.open`) and each signature the portal first reads (`form.signer.signed`) is audited with the guardian it belongs to (spec §9.4).
- A parent finds a form only through `findMyForm(ctx, id)` and `loadMyForms()` (`src/lib/forms/my-forms.ts`): within the session's family (`ctx.familyId`, only while its record is active: `activeFamilyOf()`), with no link needed, and the forms its current season asks for. Nothing is made in Documenso and no signing token is read until the signed-in guardian has agreed to the consent text of the current `CONSENT_VERSION` (`src/lib/forms/consent.ts`; `signature_consent` records who, when, the address and the version). The signing token is never stored: it is read from Documenso at each open and given only to the guardian whose recipient it is. The frame is embedded directly; its "completed" message only makes the page read the state again.
- A completed request sets `evidence_owed`; the worker (`runSignedFormsJobs()`, `src/lib/signed-forms/worker.ts`) fetches its evidence a few per wake: every item's sealed PDF, the certificate PDF, the audit log as PDF and as JSON, and the SHA-256 of each sealed and original PDF, kept in `signed_form_evidence` and `signed_form_file` with the names, signers, consents, template and company as they were, in one transaction with `forms.evidence.stored`, and only then is `evidence_owed` cleared. A failure keeps nothing and backs off (5 minutes, 30, then every 3 hours). A family or student with no QuickBooks link is no reason to wait: its evidence is kept with no copy. The one state that waits is QuickBooks connected to a company other than the roster's, where every lookup through a link answers nothing though the links exist (`recordsCompany()` null while the roster names a company), so "no link" can't be told from "can't look": before fetching anything the job checks for it (`linksReadable()`) and asks Documenso nothing; it checks again around the lookups when storing (the files are fetched by then) and keeps nothing; either way it records the request's error as `other_company`, which the Health page's forms line says in words pointing at QuickBooks, not Documenso (`evidenceHealth().otherCompany`; nothing reaches it until stage 4, since connecting another company is refused; a request still holding the old `no_link` is tried again on its own schedule); a request that moved to another family while its files were fetched is `moved_while_fetched` and is read again next wake (`EvidenceNotStored`). A later fetch with a different hash keeps the first and audits `forms.evidence.mismatch` (shown on the Health page). Evidence has no foreign key to its request and is never deleted (`KEPT_TABLES`); on a change of company it is kept, marked `earlier_company_at` and cut from its request and its app records: shown to no parent, left out of the matrix, listed in the staff Forms page's "Earlier company" archive, never matched to a family by a QuickBooks id alone. Each guardian who signed gets one copy by email (attached under 3 MB, a link otherwise: the form's page for one of several parts). A parent downloads their own at `/forms/[id]/signed`: a route handler that calls `requireParentPage()` first, looks the request up within the family (`src/lib/forms/my-evidence.ts`), answers the same 404 to anything else, and sends no-store as `signed-form.pdf`; evidence is matched by its request, the app family it names and as the current company's (`currentEvidence()`: the roster's company, or none for evidence kept while the family had no link; never another company's or an archived one). The staff Forms pages use the same rule: evidence with no company is among the current forms, never in the "Earlier company" archive, and a change of company archives it with the rest (it is never deleted). A complete form goes with a moved student (it still counts), but its PDF holds the old family's guardians: a parent sees who signed a form, and gets its PDF, only when every guardian on it is a guardian of their family (`signedWithin()`, `src/lib/forms/requests.ts`); otherwise they read "Signed" alone. Staff: the matrix `/staff/forms` (`Forms.Signature.Read`, not audited; a form with no template reads "Not ready yet", its own cell state and filter in `src/lib/signed-forms/rules.ts`, and a family whose only outstanding forms have none is offered no Remind; below it the company's other kept signed forms, an earlier season's or a departed student's, then the earlier company's), a request's and an evidence's page (audited `forms.request.read`, `forms.evidence.read` before anything is shown), and downloads at `/staff/forms/evidence/[id]/[file]` (a route handler that checks the session and permission first and audits `forms.evidence.download` before a byte is sent). "Remind now", the reminder schedule (`requirement.form_reminders`) and "Start again" (the document read from Documenso first, never withdrawn once complete, then withdrawn as the verification says, the reason kept for staff) take `Forms.Signature.ReadWrite`. A guardian who can no longer sign for a family keeps a working signing link on any document made for them, so blocking a person, and every membership closed (`closeMembership()`, `src/lib/parents/membership.ts`: the row, the person's sessions, those forms and `parent.access.ended`, in the caller's transaction; its one caller since the reconcile went is staff's "remove a guardian", `removeGuardian()` in `src/lib/parents/guardians.ts`; an open under way when either commits stores nothing for them, `goneSigners()` in `src/lib/forms/signing.ts`), starts again in the same transaction the family's unfinished forms still waiting for that guardian's signature (`formsForGoneSigner()`, `src/lib/forms/signer-gone.ts`, audited `forms.request.signer_gone`); a form they signed already is left as it is. The worker withdraws those documents and a moved student's (`form_withdrawal`) the same way: read first, a completed one left in Documenso (`form.document.withdraw.kept`), one that keeps failing audited as `form.document.withdraw.failed`, never as done. Opens are rate-limited (`form_open_parent`). Tests: `tests/helpers/documenso-stub.ts` (`startDocumensoStub()`, behaving as the verification found: a role left out stays a recipient until deleted, search by `externalId` matches substrings, both email settings recorded) and `tests/helpers/forms.ts` (`connectForms()`, `seedFormRequirement()`, `seedRequest()`, `seedConsent()`, `seedEvidence()`, `fakePdf()`; the seeds take families and students by QuickBooks id and store the app's records, `familyRecord()` and `studentRecord()`, so a test that seeds one seeds a roster company first; `requestCustomers()`, `requestByCustomer()` and `groupOf()` read a request or a Forms page's group back by customer). The browser seed never sets Documenso up, so the e2e run can't reach one; its two requests and kept evidence are stored by the app's records after 0023's fill (`scripts/seed-e2e.ts`).

### Volunteering

### Operations
- `scripts/backup.sh <folder>` dumps the portal's database and Documenso's (`BR_DOCUMENSO_DB_NAME`; a database named `documenso` when unset), checks each can be read back, keeps both or neither, keeps the newest 14 of each and records the time and whether Documenso's was in it in the setting `backup.last_success` (the Health page's backup line says so).
