## Booster Rocket: rules for agents

Booster Rocket is an open-source, self-hosted parent portal for booster clubs: each family signs in and sees only its own students, what it owes and what it must sign, while staff manage the roster. QuickBooks Online holds the club's books; the portal reads them and keeps a cache, which decides whose invoices each family sees, while the families, students and sign-ins are the portal's own records (since stage 3's slice 9c nothing read from QuickBooks changes them; a family customer added in QuickBooks comes into the portal only when a staff member who may link families to accounting makes a family from it on Reconciliation; and since slice 9d a family staff add is the portal's own record first, made in QuickBooks too only by staff who may link families to accounting, at once or later with "Create in QuickBooks and link", and a family with no link is changed in the portal only), and it writes back only what someone asks it to: a parent's own sign-in email, the roster changes staff make (new families, students, parents and members, names, a student's family, making a customer inactive), and a volunteer buy-out's invoice, made when a guardian chooses to pay instead of volunteering and voided when they switch back (Writes to QuickBooks, below). Since stage 3's slice 11a a guardian's phone and mailing address are the portal's own (The app's own records, below), and nothing sends them to QuickBooks until S11b.

### Privacy rules that are never traded
- The family comes only from the session: `ctx.familyId` (the app's own family record, `AppFamilyId`, which the session stores as that record's id, `parent_auth_session.family_id`) from `requireParentPage()` or `parentAction`. An id from the URL, a form or an action argument is untrusted: look it up within that family, and answer `notFound()` or refuse when it isn't there. A student's id is the app's, the same in every family: a lookup by a student's id is always joined to the family (`studentsStillIn()`).
- Staff-only query modules are never imported by parent code: `src/lib/roster/queries.ts` (`loadRosterPage`, `rosterFamilies`, `rosterEntry` load every family); in `src/lib/parents/directory.ts`, `listPeople`, `getPerson`, `familySignins` (the note at the top of the file); `src/lib/seasons/setup.ts`, `subjects.ts`, `staff-progress.ts`, `due-reminders.ts` and `everyone.ts` (the season's reads for every family at once: every chosen list, tick and waiver; the parent-safe reads in `recorded.ts`, `season-rows.ts` and `src/lib/families/links.ts` each take the families and students they are for, and `src/lib/families/session-family.ts` the one family it is asked about); every module of `src/lib/signed-forms/` (the matrix, evidence, reminders, start again: they read many families); `src/lib/volunteer/setup.ts`, `staff-credit.ts`, `reminders.ts` and `moved-student.ts`; `src/lib/buyouts/staff.ts`, `daily-check.ts`, `setup.ts`, `moved.ts` and `close.ts`; `src/lib/staff-home/today.ts` (the staff home's counts); `src/lib/roster/writes.ts` and `queue.ts`; `src/lib/quickbooks/sandbox-check/run.ts` and `writes.ts`; and `src/lib/families/roster-import.ts` (the import of the app's own records from the roster, below); and `src/lib/roster/no-family.ts` (the customers no family is linked to, and "Create a family from this": it reads every customer of the roster cache); and `src/lib/parents/guardians.ts` (staff add, remove and reinstate guardians: it gives any address a sign-in for any family); and `src/lib/parents/people.ts` (staff block and rename people: a rename starts sends to QuickBooks, stage 3's slice 9h); and `src/lib/parents/contact-take-in.ts` (the take-in of contact details, stage 3's slice 11a: it reads every person, guardian row, family and cached customer); and `src/lib/accounting/mapping.ts` (the accounting links screen, stage 4's slice M1: it reads every family's guardians' sign-in addresses and every customer of the roster cache). They are `STAFF_ONLY_IMPORTS` in `tests/integration/page-guards.test.ts`. A parent page reads through a query that takes the family id and returns that family alone.
- A note staff write about a family is for staff: a waiver's reason is in the audit log and on staff pages, and a parent reads only "Waived"; a volunteer credit's note or a removed check-in's reason likewise (a parent reads "Added by the club"), and a form's start-again reason is only in its audit row. `seedTwoFamilies()` gives each family such a note (`season.staffOnly`), and `tests/integration/parent-isolation.test.ts` fails a parent page that shows one, even the family's own.
- Anything stored against a QuickBooks id means another customer in another company. From stage 2 of the app-owned families spec until stage 4 ships, a different company is refused (open question 18): connecting one (`completeConnect()`, `src/lib/quickbooks/connection.ts`, reason `roster_company`, in words on the QuickBooks setup page) and reading one's roster over this one's (`refreshRoster()`, a failed run `roster_company` that says to disconnect and connect the roster's company, asking QuickBooks nothing; its entry is in `docs/runbooks/health-warnings.md`) both stop while the roster cache names another company; reconnecting the same company works as before. So nothing the portal runs reaches the clear below (when the mapping screen turns that path back on, through connect and refresh, it needs its end-to-end tests again: until stage 2 they connected a different company and read its roster; they are refusal tests now); it is kept whole for the areas that have not moved yet (their part of `tests/integration/company-change-step5.test.ts` calls it directly), and its season, volunteering (buy-outs with it) and forms parts (`clearSeasonRecords()`, then `clearVolunteerRecords()`, then `clearFormRecords()`) and the money cache (`family_money`) and the write queue (both deleted after them) run first, because those records name the app's own families and students and the database refuses to remove a record one names. The clear, inside `clearRosterOfOtherCompanyLocked()` (`src/lib/roster/source.ts`): parent access (every guardian row closed, sign-ins and welcome emails switched off; since stage 3's slice 7a no snapshot of the old company is kept, and stage 4 decides what a change of company still does to sign-ins), `family_money`, the seasons' ticks, waivers, chosen lists and due-date reminder claims (`requirement_reminder`, `clearSeasonRecords()`), the item list and the money run's place (`clearMoneyRecords()`), every volunteer sign-up, credit, reminder claim and buy-out (`clearVolunteerRecords()`, which counts the buy-outs in its audit row), every signature request with its consents and the form reminder claims and rounds (`clearFormRecords()`), parents' sessions and email change requests (`clearParentAccess()`, the first part of the clear: both name the app's own family a session chose or a change was asked in, by `restrict` keys, so they go before `clearAppRecords()`), the write queue, waiting or finished (deleted before the app's records, since each item is filed under them, and counted as `writesDeleted` in `roster.cleared`), the QuickBooks check's own records (`qbo_check_record`, `clearCheckRecords()` in `src/lib/quickbooks/sandbox-check/view.ts`; its runs, which hold no customer id, are kept), and the app's own families, students and links with each guardian row's family (`clearAppRecords()`, `src/lib/families/company-change.ts`, audited `accounting.links.cleared`; since stage 3's slice 9c nothing makes them again by itself: no roster read imports records, and stage 4's import will). A new table keyed by a QuickBooks id is cleared there too. The one exception is a signed form's evidence: it is the club's record and is never deleted (Forms, below); the clear marks it as the earlier company's and cuts it from its request and from the app's family and student it named, in one statement, before those records go. `tests/integration/company-change-step5.test.ts` proves both (a money row of each family and a queued change filed under a family and student included), and that a different company is refused and every season, volunteering, buy-out, money and forms record kept, evidence and its files included.
- QuickBooks text (names, emails, memos) is rendered as text only: never `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, never built into HTML, a URL or CSS.
- Pass a client component only what it shows: every prop is sent to the browser, rendered or not.
- Nothing family-specific is ever cached in a service worker or a shared cache. Route handlers that return data send `cache-control: no-store` (the signed-form downloads too, with a fixed file name: QuickBooks text never goes into a header). The one exception is the club's app icons (`src/app/icons/[name]/route.ts`): public, the same for everyone, and kept by their `?v=` version.
- The service worker (`public/sw.js`) stores one thing, `/offline.html`, and does one thing: it answers a page navigation that failed for lack of a connection with that page. It has one `cache.put`, no `add` or `addAll`, and no `respondWith` for anything but `mode === "navigate"`; it never stores a page, the data a page loads, an API answer or a script. Never add a caching strategy to it. `tests/unit/pwa-static.test.ts` reads the file for this, and `tests/e2e/pwa.spec.ts` signs a parent in, browses, and checks the caches hold `/offline.html` and nothing else. `public/offline.html` is the same for every club and person: no club name, no family data, no script.
- No secret reaches the browser: `scripts/check-client-bundle.mjs` searches the built client files for the secret values. Never read `process.env` or `@/env` in a client component.

### The app's own records (stage 1 of the app-owned families spec)
- `family` and `student` hold the portal's own families and students, `guardian.family_id` each membership's family, and `accounting_link` the tie of each to its QuickBooks customer: `system` (`quickbooks`), `realm_id` (the company), `customer_id`, and exactly one of `family_id`, `student_id` or `guardian_id`, which is the link's kind. ### Seasons
- A season has steps, a step has requirements, and progress is one `requirement_progress` row per requirement and family or student (a tick, a waiver with its reason, or both). ### Forms (Documenso)
- Forms are signed in the club's own Documenso (v1 spec §9 as corrected by `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-documenso-verification.md`). `src/lib/documenso/client.ts` is its only client: server only, a timeout on every call, errors as short codes, nothing logged, no token in any message. In the code they are `AppFamilyId`, `AppStudentId` and `SubjectId` (`subjectOfRequest()`, `requestRecords()` in `src/lib/forms/requests.ts`); new audit rows name `familyId` and `studentId` in their body (older rows keep the QuickBooks ids, and the audit page's record filter finds both: `formRowsOf()` in `src/lib/audit/query.ts`, never for a kept form's rows). - One `signature_request` per form requirement and subject (family or student); its id is the document's `externalId`. A document that seems gone (404, or another `externalId`) resets only an unsigned request; one that holds a signature (`holdsSignature()`) is kept and asked about again, the 404 counts as a failure on the Health page, and an open that finds such a document of the request by `externalId` takes it up again (`form.document.recover`) rather than refusing. Each hand-out of a signing frame (`form.sign.open`) and each signature the portal first reads (`form.signer.signed`) is audited with the guardian it belongs to (spec §9.4).
- A parent finds a form only through `findMyForm(ctx, id)` and `loadMyForms()` (`src/lib/forms/my-forms.ts`): within the session's family (`ctx.familyId`, only while its record is active: `activeFamilyOf()`), with no link needed, and the forms its current season asks for. - A completed request sets `evidence_owed`; the worker (`runSignedFormsJobs()`, `src/lib/signed-forms/worker.ts`) fetches its evidence a few per wake: every item's sealed PDF, the certificate PDF, the audit log as PDF and as JSON, and the SHA-256 of each sealed and original PDF, kept in `signed_form_evidence` and `signed_form_file` with the names, signers, consents, template and company as they were, in one transaction with `forms.evidence.stored`, and only then is `evidence_owed` cleared. A failure keeps nothing and backs off (5 minutes, 30, then every 3 hours). A family or student with no QuickBooks link is no reason to wait: its evidence is kept with no copy. The one state that waits is QuickBooks connected to a company other than the roster's, where every lookup through a link answers nothing though the links exist (`recordsCompany()` null while the roster names a company), so "no link" can't be told from "can't look": before fetching anything the job checks for it (`linksReadable()`) and asks Documenso nothing; it checks again around the lookups when storing (the files are fetched by then) and keeps nothing; either way it records the request's error as `other_company`, which the Health page's forms line says in words pointing at QuickBooks, not Documenso (`evidenceHealth().otherCompany`; nothing reaches it until stage 4, since connecting another company is refused; a request still holding the old `no_link` is tried again on its own schedule); a request that moved to another family while its files were fetched is `moved_while_fetched` and is read again next wake (`EvidenceNotStored`).
